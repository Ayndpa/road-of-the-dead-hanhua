**Soldiers** are a type of enemy in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")'s FUBAR [gamemode](./gamemodes "Gamemodes"), along with being allied NPCs in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")'s main campaign.

## Overview

They are soldiers of the [Military](./military "Military") ordered to contain the [zombie threat](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus") and enforce quarantine on [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") after day 5 of the outbreak. As martial law has been declared, the soldiers have been ordered to shoot anyone on sight who may be attempting to exit the city, along with anyone who appears to have been infected.

Soldiers wear a green camouflage uniform, gloves, and ballistic armor that allows them to resist gunfire. Additionally, they wear a gas mask (presumably a M50 judging by the snouts) to prevent them from being exposed to air potentially contaminated with the infection. In [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") their lens are black, while in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") they are bright orange.

Military personnel make use of the M16 automatic rifle when on-foot, but will drop it and switch to their sidearm (presumably a Glock based on its appearance) when clinging to the hood of a vehicle. Additionally, they operate spikestrips and landmines in an attempt to stop anyone from leaving [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

In [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), it is implied that they also operate M60 general-purpose machineguns, MP5 submachine guns, Mossberg 500 shotguns, and MAC-10 machinepistols, as they are found scattered throughout the city at police stations, derelict supply crates, and vehicles looted by the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") during their escape.

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")

In [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"), soldiers are the main force used by the [Military](./military "Military") to prevent [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") from escaping [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

Though littered throughout the levels as a whole, soldiers are most numerous at the checkpoints between each district. These checkpoints are indicated by radio transmissions and large amounts of fences and military equipment as John approaches them. As time goes on, the soldiers adopt different tactics and begin utilizing different gear in an attempt to stop his car, before eventually being ordered to pull out of [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") and are absent from the West Ridge and the Tunnel Drive entirely.

There are 5 phenotypes of soldiers encountered in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"):

- **Static** soldiers will stand in one place and never move, firing at John as he enters their line of vision and until he passes. To avoid being hit, simply stay out of their line of fire.

- **Aligning** soldiers will dynamically move around the highway as John approaches before standing in one spot and opening fire on his vehicle. To avoid being hit, either strike them as they run across the highway with your car or simply avoid them.

- **Hidden** soldiers will hide behind vehicles and debris, popping out from the sides as John approaches them and opening fire on his car. Be careful, as hidden soldiers are usually spotted together and will attempt to force John to swerve into the path of other soldiers or [greater](./hellfire "Hellfire") [threats](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet").

- **Spike** soldiers will crouch on the furthest lanes of the highway and operate spike strips, dragging them into the center lane as John passes in an attempt to blow out his tires. Simply swerve out of the spikes' path, as the soldiers themselves are mostly defenseless, however if ran into at low speeds they will still cling to your windshield like regular enemies. But this is extremely unlikely, so don't worry about it. As with hidden soldiers, beware that they may be trying to force you to swerve into bigger enemies.

- **Bomb** soldiers will crouch on the furthest lanes of the highway similarly to spike soldiers, operating strips of landmines that will detonate as John approaches. As with the former, they are completely defenseless on their own, and do not make a direct attempt to engage the player. Swerve towards where the explosions originate from to avoid being damaged. They can also cling to your windshield but again, highly unlikely.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

#### Lost Guns

Soldiers do not appear in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") outside of cutscenes, but bomb and spike squads are implied to have passed through many of the areas that the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") visit during their escape.

Upon reaching Flyboy One at Fairview Mall, the duo encounter Sherman's bodyguards, who have little dialogue but serve as friendly NPCs (implied to be covering the east side of the helicopter, opposite of where the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") are). Later, after they escape the city, the bodyguard duo prevent [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") from getting up to attack [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") after news of the nuke failing to contain the infection.

The last we see of them is when they, Sherman, and the Lost Guns discuss what to do next as they begin flying off towards Alliance to take back the city, fortify it, and defend it from the zombies.

#### FUBAR

Meanwhile, they return in the FUBAR gamemode as enemies attempting to prevent the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") from leaving [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

They function identically to how they do in the first game, though can now be shot and killed by the duo and take considerably less damage than zombies. Alternatively, they can also be suppressed by gunfire, spooking them and causing them to retreat to find cover. Also, zombies and mutants that make contact with a soldier will now instantly kill them.

Soldiers will now also vocalize when the player stops at a point of interest to complete a task, spawning around the sides of buildings and attempting to siege [Diane](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") while the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") is stopped.

Despite their low health and the ability to be dispatched at a distance, [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")'s lack of health regeneration makes them an incredibly large threat. One or two can easily kill a careless player who doesn't see them in time. To counteract this, bring as many medshots and steroids as you can into FUBAR, as you likely won't have many opportunities to restock.

Also of note:

- **Spike** soldiers are now more intelligent and will pull the spikestrip into the lane the player is driving on, requiring them to actually make an effort to dodge the spikes. Additionally, they can be shot before they can pull the strip, causing it to not move.

- **Bomb** soldiers can be shot before they detonate the mines, causing them to not go off.

## Trivia

- Soldiers, instead of leaping on the player like zombies, will fire their pistol one shot at a time at [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") or the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") while clinging to their hood.
  - Oddly, the pistol used appears more akin to a [Glock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glock "wikipedia:Glock"), and not the M9 used by the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2").

- In [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")'s FUBAR mode, soldiers that collide with zombies will be instantly killed.

- A zombie/mutant and a soldier can cling to the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") at the same time in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), creating a strange effect as they both prioritize the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") over each other.

- Oddly, soldiers in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") will always refer to their target as 'he', even when [Diane](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") is driving. Additionally, they identify themselves as 'Highway Units' even when not on [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65").
  - Of course, this is because they recycled lines from the first game.

- The soldiers' armor seems ridiculously ineffective, as they quickly die to a few shots anywhere on their body in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), even from low-caliber weaponry like the SMGs and pistols that would likely be stopped by their vests.
  - This was likely done for balancing reasons, as the soldiers already do considerable damage to the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") even with their low health.

- Soldiers have unique taunt lines when clinging to the hood of the player's car, ordering them to stop the car and threatening to kill them. They also have unique lines for being knocked off the car by a collision, mostly consisting of screaming.

- Soldiers in both games can shoot through vehicles, creating an annoying effect where the player cannot see a soldier but is still hit, unable to retaliate outside of switching lanes. Also applies to [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), now with the added effect of the player being unable to fire back.

- As evidenced by idle dialogue in the garage, the soldiers were ordered to begin firing at civilians on sight in addition to shooting those who requested aid. This seems to explain why the [civilians](./civilians "Civilians") are running around the streets instead of taking shelter at any of the highway checkpoints.

- Interestingly, the soldiers do not use codewords or phrasing in their radio transmissions throughout both games (screwing them over once [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") gets access to their channel).
  - While obviously done for gameplay reasons, it is also explained in one of [Road of the Dead's](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") idle dialogues, where a soldier is ordered to "forget the freakin' code, son" as they're fighting "dead people".
