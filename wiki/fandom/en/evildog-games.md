EvilDog Games is the game-development studio responsible for developing the [Saga of the Dead](./saga-of-the-dead-wiki "Saga of The Dead Wiki") games.

EvilDog Games was founded and lead by main programmer [Evil-Dog](./evil-dog "Evil-dog").
