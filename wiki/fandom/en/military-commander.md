The **Military Commander** is an unseen supporting character in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"). Initially in charge of the [Military](./military "Military") response to the outbreak in [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"), he is eventually replaced by [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") after repeated failures to stop both [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") and the [zombies](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus").

## Biography

From what can be inferred by his job during both of the games, the Military Commander is likely an experienced member of the [Military](./military "Military") with a high rank (though probably only that of a Major or Colonel).

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")

The Military Commander is initially in charge of the outbreak as far back as Day 5, when the outbreak occurs and the [Military](./military "Military") quarantine is fully put into place. He can be heard talking to [another soldier](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") on the radio, attempting to remind him that they are trained to [handle](./john-creasman "John Creasman") [anything](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus") (though the uneasiness in his voice suggests [otherwise](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle")).

Idle chatter in the garage suggests that the commander is quite brutal and by the book with how he deals with the outbreak (like how he orders a group of soldiers to begin opening fire on [Civilians](./civilians "Civilians")), though seems to disregard important parts of protocol (such as when he reprimands a soldier for using codewords over the radio, saying they're dealing with zombies) and even outright endangers groups of [soldiers](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") by denying evacuations and requests to retreat to better positions.

When [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") begins his escape from the city, he is almost immediately alerted when the first checkpoint is rammed through by a black sportscar with tinted glass. As time goes on, he grows more and more frustrated and ends up dedicating more and more [Military](./military "Military") resources to the apprehension of Creasman, even when outposts elsewhere are rapidly being overrun and their attempts to stop him are repeatedly and consistently ending in failure.

By the time John reaches Chinatown, someone higher in the [Military](./military "Military") chain of command decides that he is no longer fit to assume the leading role due to his failures, and [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") takes command of the operation instead. Regardless, he is presumably left in some smaller commanding role during the rest of the outbreak, as he can still be heard ordering [soldiers](./military-soldier "Military Soldier"), [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire"), and [air support](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") for the rest of the game.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

The Military Commander is heard again in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") during the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns'](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") attempt to escape [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"). Most of his lines are recycled from the first game, and he never directly speaks to either of them.

In the ending cutscene, the [Military](./military "Military") headquarters that was in charge of the [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") quarantine is overrun. The chances of the commander surviving are incredibly low, considering every other soldier we hear that was stationed there is killed or infected by the zombies, and as such it can be presumed that he was either infected or killed during the chaos.

## Personality

The Military Commander, as stated above, is initially unempathetic and seems to not care very much for the [soldiers](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") inside [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"), much less the [Civilians](./civilians "Civilians"). Additionally, as time goes on and the [Military](./military "Military") repeatedly fails to stop [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), he grows more and more angry and even insults the soldiers' skills, reprimanding them for being unable to fulfill what is supposedly such a simple task.

Nonetheless, another side of his personality can be seen as the game goes on: the realization that the [Military](./military "Military") is in over their heads and that they're not prepared for [the](./john-creasman "John Creasman") [situation](./common-undead "Common Undead") [at](./ravager "Ravager") [hand](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies").

Even in the intro, he can be heard giving an uncertain message of encouragement to one soldier over the radio, and later on he begins to panic and give incorrect or downright nonsensical orders to the [ground troops](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") inside the city as he begins to realize that the situation is rapidly spiraling out of control (like when he tells a group of soldiers to retreat to the Chinatown Plaza, an area that they had already come from, and then panics and tells the soldiers to "just move freaking north").

## Trivia

- The Military Commander is voiced by the real life John Creasman, who also voices Captain Walker in [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead").

- As stated above, the incident involving HQ being overrun at the end of [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") leaves his fate quite grim. The most likely outcome is that he was infected, as it is incredibly unlikely he managed to fight his way through the zombies and escape into the desert.

- Despite being replaced by [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") halfway through the first game, he can still be heard ordering [soldiers](./military-soldier "Military Soldier"), [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire"), and [Bomber Jets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") until the end of the game.
  - While this was definitely done for gameplay reasons, an equally likely alternative is that he was demoted to only having charge of individual units instead of making decisions involving the entire operation.
