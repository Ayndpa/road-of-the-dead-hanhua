The **Bomber Jet** is a multirole fighter jet used by the [Military](./military "Military") during the events of [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") to conduct carpet bombings on designated targets.

## Gameplay

### Road of the Dead

Bomber Jets make persistent appearances after the player passes the sixth checkpoint at Chinatown, and after around 12km in Police State.

Telegraphed by the pilot requesting permission to engage whilst flying over John, the jet will turn around and go in a straight, right or left line down a lane of the highway, carpet bombing it until it passes. Zombies, civilians, and soldiers caught in the path of a bombing will be gibbed; John's car will take incredible damage if it gets struck.

If a jet bombing left or right side, you must immediately move to the safe zone, which begins at the second yellow highway marking line from the bombing site. If straight, you can only escape by pulling onto the sidewalk.

If you drive at speeds below 40 km/h, the jet will not be able to hit a car. However, this may leave the player vulnerable to other dangers, such as [Bomb Units](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") or [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire").

After a flyover, the jet will give HQ a sitrep:

- **If the player is hit**, the pilot will express brief excitement before realizing John is still going. HQ will order him to return to base. This resets the spawn timer.

- **If the player avoids the bombing**, the pilot will tell HQ the player is still going and say one of two things:
  - **Nothing**, at which point HQ will order him to circle around and hit John again. This results in an immediate respawn and can repeat up to 2 times until he is out of ammo.
  - **He is out of ammo**, causing the pilot to disengage and return to base for a refill of his weaponry. This resets the spawn timer and prevents him from immediately conducting another strike.

### Road of the Dead 2

Bomber Jets make a second appearance in Road of the Dead 2 after [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") reach Juneau Street.

Bomber Jets function identically compared how they did in the first game, though they no longer acknowledge the player. On the other hand, Diane and Cocheta will typically express shock at seeing the jets fly over them, telegraphing their attack to the player.

A unique variant of the carpet bombing occurs when reaching the Juneau Street chapter. Dispatch will order the jets to initiate a carpet bombing 'west of Juneau Street' as the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") try and fail to call it off. The ensuing carpet bombing is unique in that it consists of five jets back to back, an occurrence that happens at no other point in the series.

Jets will also blow up vehicles, [mines](./mines "Mines"), [zombies](./common-undead "Common Undead") ([mutants](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") included), and props that happen to be caught in their path. Be especially careful during segments set on [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") or in industrial areas, as carpet bombings can eliminate resources (like ammunition and water) or set off chain reactions of explosions that can injure or kill the player.

In the FUBAR gamemode, Bomber Jets function identically to their campaign versions, though they now use their lines from the first game that request permission to engage before flying over. Additionally, [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire") can be baited into the path of these carpet bombings to instantly force him into retreating for an emergency landing.

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")

During the events of the first game, [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") dispatches bomber jets to conduct strikes against [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") after he successfully passes the sixth military checkpoint at Chinatown. Persistent for the rest of the game, they grow more numerous and continue to pursue the player even after all other military personnel have been killed or started to evacuate the area.

In the Police State gamemode, Jets begin to make their appearance around the 12km mark, and will continue to spawn until the player is killed.

A unique jet named [Phantom Eagle](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") is mentioned during the events of the game once John reaches the Tunnel Drive, given an order by both the President and [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") to drop the nuclear warhead that eviscerates [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

During the events of Road of the Dead 2, bomber jets once again show up as indirect opposition to [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") as they attempt to escape Evans City.

As the duo make it to Juneau Street, dispatch sends a squadron of bomber jets to carpet bomb an area west of it. Unfortunately being right in the path of the jets, Diane attempts to call off the bombing to no avail, and the duo are faced with 5 jets flying over and carpet bombing the street back-to-back. Afterward, the duo are continually faced with jets striking areas near them until the end of the campaign.

Additionally, [Phantom Eagle](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") returns as a cameo, overheard talking to [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") in a cameo that mirrors the same scene from the first game.

#### Survival Guide Description

Near the final days and hours, the military, in full panic, issued the use of aircraft to bomb large parts of Evans City. In this vain attempt to stop the undead plague from spreading, many innocent civilians were caught in those jets bombings while trying to escape the city or simply by being held up in buildings in the bombing' path.

During the breakdown of communications, the jets are known to accidentally bomb other military personnel who happen to be in the wrong place at the wrong time. The jets can only bomb in a straight line so **SWERVE** out of bombing lane or stay out of that lane if you see a jet flying over it. Their bombs can cause chain reaction explosions, so be aware of your surroundings.
