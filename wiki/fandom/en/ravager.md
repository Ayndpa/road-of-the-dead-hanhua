### Ravager

**Ravagers** are a slightly less common undead type, its name coined by some military personnel for being 'ravaged', half-eaten and rotten looking. These undead are slightly mutated and suffer horrible wounds from being eaten when they were killed originally.

They tend to be slightly stronger than common undead and are able to take more punishment before being destroyed.

## Gameplay

Ravager is a type of zombie in [*Road of The Dead*](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead") and [*Road of The Dead 2*](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2"). The Ravager is a stronger type of [Common Undead](./common-undead "Common Undead") which in Road of the Dead start appearing on the third level and *Road of the Dead 2* at the very beginning of the game. In the first game, Ravagers are simply an additional model for Common Undead, and have no special abilities.

It's nickname, "Ravager", originates from some of the military personnel because of their half-eaten skin. This zombie is similar to the Common Undead, except having more health.

Curiously, the second female zombie in the Common Undead class (unique to *Road of the Dead 2*, identified through its more realistic art style versus the comic-book-like bold outlines of other common zombies, and its seemingly gnawed-away skin around the jaw) appears to be as resilient as a Ravager, and acts similarly. This may be an error.

When a Ravager grapples your hood, it appears to have two phases. The first phase is calm motion, about 2/3 of its time on the hood. The second phase is a violent gyration on the hood, making headshots more difficult. This gyration is a critical red flag to kill it NOW, or blow off an arm. After blowing off an arm, the Ravager briefly enters this phase in response before returning to the first phase. Failure to end the second phase in time will result in attack.
