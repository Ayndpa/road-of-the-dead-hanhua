| Upgrade Name | Cost | Effects | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tires |  | Improves vehicle handling and resilience against [Spike Strips](./spike-strips "Spike Strips"), [Corpses](./corpses "Corpses") and [Oil Spills](./oil-spills "Oil Spills"). | |

### Weapon Stats

##### All weapon stats are exclusive to its user per weapon.

| Upgrade Name | Cost | Effects |

| Capacity |  | Increases the maximum amount of ammo held in the weapon and reserves. |
