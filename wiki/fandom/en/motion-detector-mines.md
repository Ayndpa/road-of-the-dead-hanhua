Motion Detector Mines

 

These mines are similar to the normal [mines](./mines "Mines") except they emit a green light instead of a red one. They will be lined up across the street and explode similarly to [Bomb Squads](./enemy-soldier#Bomb_Squads "Enemy Soldier") when you get too close to them in [your vehicle](./humvee "Humvee").

Swerving slightly towards the start of the chain explosion can help dodge it.

They can also be blown up by gun fire.
