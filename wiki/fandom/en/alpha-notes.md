In [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead"), the player can find old papers, notes and documents left over by [Alpha Team](./alpha-team "Alpha Team"), which with the [Alpha Tapes](./alpha-tapes "Alpha Tapes"), helps shed light on the story and origin of the [Zombie Virus](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus").

The following are all of the avaliable notes that can be found in the game:

## Killing the Specimen

*This note seems to be part of some notebook, possibly from one of the doctors.*

Because the virus focuses on keeping the brain alive, destroying the brain will in fact terminate the specimen. So severing the head or perforating the brain, for example, with a pistol shot, WILL "kill" the specimen.

There's no point doing research with a "dead" specimen so at this point, you have to get a new specimen.

## Infectious Symptoms

*This note seems to be part of documentation on the S14 virus*

#### S14 Specifications

Project: Flatline

Leader: [Dr. Lori Benedict](./dr-lori-benedict "Dr. Lori Benedict")

Subject: Infection Symptoms

Full infection from the S14 virus can take as long as 3 days and as little as 20 minutes depending on the rate of blood contamination. Once the virus gets in the blood stream, the symptoms will include fever, sharp muscle pain, loss of sensitivity, blurry vision, heat waves, paralysis and more.

The virus is 100% lethal so any attempt to stop or slowdown the infection is useless.

## Item Knowledge

*This note seems to be a guide from one of the doctors experimenting on the specimen. This note is a tutorial on how to research objects*

In order to trigger all possible reactions, it's important to know what you're working with. Doing research on items by pressing and holding them will reveal the criterias necessary to trigger the different reactions.

Being aware of the specimen's mood, humanity and hunger factors is crucial to trigger the right reactions.

## Hygiene Protocol

*This note seems to be a memo to be distributed to all research personnel*

To be distributed to the entire research staff

Hygiene protocols must be applied to their full extent. Due to the very dangerous nature of the S14 virus, full isolation gear must be used and any contact with a specimen must be followed by a complete decontamination to ensure that there are no traces of S14 outside the laboratory.

Every instruments must be steriilized after each operation and all the clothes washed, no exception

## Specimen Individuality

*This note seems to be another in the guide from one of the doctors, this time shedding light on the specimen's psychology and how researching specimen could help incite responses*

The specimen are not very different physiologically, only in appearance. But deep down in their psyche, there are differences based on who they were before the infection.

Researching the specimen's background will reveal such nuances in its psychology. You can find items for which the specimen will have more intense responses.

## Infected Behaviour

*This note seems to be another part of the documentation on the S14 virus*

#### S14 Specifications

Project: Flatline

Leader: Dr. Lori Benedict

Subject: Infected Behaviour

Once fully infected and brought back to life, the infected person will mostly seek one thing, to consume raw flesh, preferably human flesh. All sensory inputs seem to be cut off so influences like pain, light and electric current will not be a deterrent.

Deal with infected people with caution as they will be very aggressive.

## Advanced Reactions

*Another page in the zombie experimental guide detailing advanced reactions*

To trigger an advanced reaction with an item, it is crucial to fully research (press and hold the item in the items list) the item and trigger all its basic reactions first. By doing the full research the requirements to trigger the advanced reaction will be revealed. The advanced reactions gives a lot of research points.
