**Creasman**: Huh?

**Broadcaster**: This is an emergency alert system broadcast. This is not a test.

(The alarm continues. Then cuts after a few seconds)

**Newscaster**: Hello, citizen. Due to unforeseen and unexplained events, the city has been quarantined. Please stay home. Lock all your doors and windows. Stay out of sight until further notice. Stay home until further notice.

(The alarm continues. Chaos ensures outside his garage. Zombies groan, people screams, gunshots ring out. Creasman is shocked.)

**Creasman**: (Thinking) Whoa. This is bad...

**Female Newscaster**: This is not an isolated case-
