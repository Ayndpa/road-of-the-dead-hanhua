Army Vehicles

 

The military are stationed at many checkpoints and locations throughout [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") to uphold the strict quarantine in hopes to contain the mysterious and deadly epidemic.

As the days went by, more control was lost along with military personnel. Their vehicles lay [abandoned](./abandoned-vehicles "Abandoned Vehicles") throughout.

Intact vehicles have a small portion of ammunition within them that can be scavenged.
