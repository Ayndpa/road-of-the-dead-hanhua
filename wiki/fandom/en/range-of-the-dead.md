***Range of the Dead*** is a 3D zombie shooter, you’ll have to deal with gravity, the target’s distance and varying wind to survive this undead filled apocalypse. You have a selection of weapons to deal with the zombies. You play as [Allen C. Tyler](./allen-c-tyler "Allen C. Tyler") in this story sequel to [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead"). He has now left the laboratory and is heading toward the closest town called Senoia. Before he can embark on the perilous journey, he must hone his rifle skills so he can survive.

## Gameplay

The gameplay is simple to learn but may be difficult to master. The game requires you to shoot zombies, preferably in the head for a clean instant kill, from a distance. You must account for the bullet drop and wind variation which become more of a problem as the more zombies you kill.

The further the target is, the higher you have to aim to account for the bullet drop and gravity.

The stronger the wind, the more you have to aim in the opposite direction of the wind. That is, if the wind is blowing left, you have to aim further right from the target and vice versa.

The further the distance, the higher you'll have to aim above your target to account for gravity dragging the bullet down.

The main campaign involves 100 levels each of which offers one zombie to kill, but remember to account for the wind and distance.

There are also 6 [gamemodes](./gamemodes "Gamemodes") to play with such as:

- **Limited:** Where you must kill as many zombies as you can with a limited amount of ammunition. You start with 3, killing zombies with a headshot will give you 4 extra bullets, killing them otherwise only gives 3.

- **Body parts:** Hit the zombies body part indicated by the game, you have 20 misses to hit as many part as possible.

- **Hurricane:** The wind constantly varies in speed and direction in this gamemode, there is no gravity and your influence over the bullet is much higher. Kill as many zombies possible until you miss 5 times.

- **Survival:** You can no longer influence the bullet in the air, get as many kills you can until you fail 10 times.

- **Eagle Eye:** You must headshot as many zombies you can in a row. A single fail and it's over.

- **Grenade:** Kill zombies from a distance by throwing grenades. You start with 5, a first try kill give you back 2 grenades, otherwise only 1.

## Trivia

- After killing all 100 zombies in the main campaign and finishing it, a cutscene will play where a [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire") helicopter will fly over Allen. Allen realises that there are still other survivors and not all hope is loss. Where there is a military helicopter, there's a military base.
- It is not possible to throw a grenade over the distance the game gives in real life.
