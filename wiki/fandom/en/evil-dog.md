Marco Arsenault, also known as **Evil-Dog**, is a Canadian game developer behind the Saga of the Dead series. He resides in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal), [Quebec, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec).

## Games

- [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead")
- [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2")
- [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead")
- [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of The Dead")
- [Segway of the Dead](./segway-of-the-dead-2 "Segway of The Dead")
- [Streets of the Dead](./streets-of-the-dead "Streets of the Dead") (upcoming)
