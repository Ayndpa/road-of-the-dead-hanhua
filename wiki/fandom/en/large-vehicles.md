Large Vehicles

 

Throughout [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"), there are many large vehicles, left abandoned. From schoolbuses to large military trucks. These vehicles may cause more damage from crashing into them than other vehicle types.

Using your SWERVE ability to dodge them can be effective, due to their large size that can take up much of a street.

These very large vehicles can be blown up much like other vehicles, with rockets or other explosions, but only chunks of the vehicle at a time.

Large vehicles may include: school buses, semi-truck, fire engine
