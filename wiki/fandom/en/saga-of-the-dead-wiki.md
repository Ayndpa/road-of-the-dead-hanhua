# **Welcome to the Saga of the Dead Wiki!**

We're a collaborative community website about the Saga of the Dead game series developed by [Evil-Dog Games](./evildog-games "EvilDog Games") that anyone, including you, can build and expand. Wikis like this one depend on readers getting involved and adding content. Click the "ADD NEW PAGE" or "EDIT" button at the top of any page to get started!

Saga of the Dead is a series of correlated video games that revolves around a [zombie virus](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus") that quickly spreads through the country, overrunning major population centres in less than a month. The first of which was [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") which fell in a single week and more soon followed. The games primarily take the perspective of different characters for each game but tend to make references or show cameos of previous protagonists.

## [Segway of the Dead](./segway-of-the-dead-2 "Segway of the Dead")

 ![Segway Icon](/fandom-img/Segway_Icon.png) 

 
> "With only a segway vehicle, you have to survive the zombie apocalypse. Start with the story mode where you'll go through an emotion stirring series of 200 action-packed and gut wrenching missions. Hope you enjoy this April Fools Day addition to the Saga of the Dead."

Segway of the Dead is a side scroller April Fool's joke that has no relevance to the ongoing Saga of the Dead story. The game follows a Rambo imitator whose name is "Johnny" using a segway to avoid the zombies in Evans City. The game was designed to be unplayable as the first level is impossible to complete. and yields no points to upgrade the vehicle.

## [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

 ![ROTD 2](/fandom-img/ROTD_2.png) 

 
> "The long awaited sequel to Road of the Dead is here and it's HUGE!  Survive the massive outbreak in Evans City as two soldiers trying to escape the city against all odds."

Road of the Dead 2 is a (genre) game occurring coincidentally with Road of the Dead. The game revolves around 2 soldiers, [Diane](./diane "Diane") and [Cocheta](./cocheta "Cocheta") disobeying military orders and fleeing Evans City in their humvee before the city is completely overrun. The gameplay is similar to Road of the Dead but with more zombies and obstacles, resource management and with a variety of firearms to use from the relative safety of your humvee to mow down the enemies in your way.

## [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of the Dead")

 ![RaOTD](/fandom-img/RaOTD.png) 

 
> "Hone your rifle skills to survive the zombie apocalypse from a safe distance. You'll have to deal with gravity, the target's distance and varying wind in 6 fun game modes."

Range of the Dead is a shooting gallery game revolving around returning protagonist Allen C. Tyler from Lab of the Dead, now leaving his laboratory behind for the town of Senoia. Allen shoots down zombies in the far off valley at a safe distance, honing his rifle skills in preparation for his perilous journey.

## Getting the Adobe Flash Standalone Projector

The Adobe Flash Standalone Projector is a very simple and lightweight application. It does not need to be installed like a standard application, and can be placed in any folder on your computer.

### Windows

[Download](https://archive.org/download/flashplayer32_0r0_363_win_sa/flashplayer32_0r0_363_win_sa.exe) the executable.

### macOS

[Download](https://archive.org/download/flashplayer32_0r0_363_win_sa/flashplayer32_0r0_363_mac_sa.dmg) the .dmg file. Open the .dmg file to mount it. Drag the `Flash Player.app` file out into a folder on your computer.

### Linux

[Download](https://archive.org/download/flashplayer32_0r0_363_win_sa/flashplayer32_0r0_363_linux_sa.x86_64.tar.gz) the .tar.gz file. Extract the file. Run the `Flash Player` executable.
