Driving Skills

 

Both Diane and Cocheta can level up individual skills as they use them. The more each character drives, the better they will get at it.

They will also get better Toughness as they survive impacts and explosions, making them better suited to handle collisions, explosions and [mutant](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") side grapples.
