**Civilians** are noncombatants left inside [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") during the outbreak, encountered in both [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2").

## Overview

Civilians are innocent bystanders of various backgrounds still stuck inside Evans City during the outbreak and events of both [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"). As most of their vehicles were crashed, abandoned in traffic, or stopped by the [Military](./military "Military") during the initial quarantine, most civilians found in the games are running around.

Civilians wear generic clothing and have 7 variants, ranging from casual clothing to business suits. Judging by the lack of injuries and their panicked state, it is implied that the civilians found in both games have only recently failed their attempts to escape and happen to be frantically running through the areas that [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") and the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") are passing through.

If the player passes a civilian, they can be heard screaming and panicking, fading away into the distance as the player drives away. Civilians in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") express gratitude and thankfulness to the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") when rescued by the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee").

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")

Civilians are encountered in large numbers during [John Creasman's](./john-creasman "John Creasman") escape on [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65"), fleeing from both the [Military](./military "Military"), who have orders to shoot them on sight, and the [zombies](./common-undead "Common Undead") roaming the highway. They are present in all levels and gamemodes.

Civilians will randomly run across the highway in a zig-zag motion, generally oblivious [to](./john-creasman "John Creasman") [their](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") [surroundings](./common-undead "Common Undead"). As with all non-player characters in the game, they can be killed by [Bomber Jets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") carpet bombing their lane. Though they cannot be rescued, they will flee from the player if the horn is blown in their vicinity (distance increases with horn upgrade level).

Civilians die instantly when hit by [the car](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car"), even at extremely low speeds. Player-induced civilian casualties will cause them to lose RP, multiplying depending on how many the player hits during a given run.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

Another type of civilian has been added in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"): [Moving Cars](./moving-cars "Moving Cars"). Though they only spawn at intersections inside [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"), they pose a threat to the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") as their unpredictable behavior can cause them to collide with their vehicle. There is no point loss for hitting a civilian vehicle, but it will deal damage to the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee").

Standard civilians found running throughout the streets can now be rescued by stopping next to them and blasting the horn at them. Assuming that the player currently does not have [hood](./common-undead "Common Undead") [clingers](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies"), the civilian will run to the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") and climb inside, thanking the duo with a short voiceline and point bonus. They can be dropped off at [Police Stations](./police-stations "Police Stations") and [Churches](./churches "Churches") for additional point bonuses.

The [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") is only able to transport five civilians at a given time. Hitting the limit will prompt one of the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") to comment that the vehicle is full, and any civilians encountered until slots are freed up will ignore the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") entirely. They functionally identically in all gamemodes outside of Rescue Op.

In Rescue Op, civilians are inside [Police Stations](./police-stations "Police Stations") and [Churches](./churches "Churches") and must be rescued by the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon"), who are riding a helicopter as its door gunners. [zombies](./common-undead "Common Undead") not dealt with quickly enough can enter the shelter, killing a civilian or two before being removed from play. Once all zombies have been slain, any surviving civilians will leave and head for the helicopter. If they all perish, the player's current attempt ends and they must restart.

## Survival Guide Description

![Civilians](/fandom-img/Civilians.png)

The few civilians remaining alive in [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") are in a last desperate attempt to escape the plague after the military quarantine failed and power structures fell apart.

Many tried to leave in their cars but were blocked at gunpoint on the highways and streets by military personnel. During the final chaos, many abandoned their cars, trapped in traffic and panic, tried to escape the oncoming hordes of undead, running anywhere they could, but most often to their death.

## Trivia

- [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") is a civilian as well, though he has clearly put more intricate care and thought into his escape plan compared to the frantic running of the civilians encountered across [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") and [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65").

- The reason for civilians never being encountered within military checkpoints is that the [Military Soldiers](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") have orders to shoot ('neutralize') all civilians on sight, including those already within checkpoints.

- Like [Common Undead](./common-undead "Common Undead"), if they are hit centerwise, they will splatter into various organs and bones, noticeably without any trace of flesh or skin. These are counted as gib kills.

- The Survival Guide entry depicts two unused civilian models, both slightly adjusted versions of civilians that appear in-game. One is a Caucasian man who uses the assets of the first male African American civilian's suit and a slight recolor of the male Caucasian civilians, with dark brown hair and a mustache, while the other is an African American woman, who is essentially a palette swap of the second female civilian.

- The civilians, as well as the unseen drivers of the [moving cars](./moving-cars "Moving Cars"), are the only characters with uncredited voice actors.
  - Althoug uncredited, the idle lines from RotD (which are reused in RotD) can be identified as having been provided by Dreux Ferrano Jr. and "Sapphire". The new rescue lines and the moving car drivers in RotD 2 are voiced by Chris Guerrero (male) and "Beckie" (female).

- Although emergency workers are technically considered civilians and their vehicles do appear, they are never seen at any point in the game, implying they were either killed off or pulled out.
