Military personnel that has been killed trying to keep the quarantine upheld in Evans City. They can be seen all around the city, but more concentrated in overrun checkpoints.

They are on par with Brutes in toughness and speed but they still wear their military gear and armor, providing more protection. Their helmet and body armor have a small chance to cause bullets to ricochet off them, dealing zero damage.

## Gameplay

The Undead Soldier is a type of zombie appearing only in [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2"). Before being infected, they were soldiers of the Pennsylvania Army National Guard.

It is advised to shoot them in the head as fast as you can, otherwise, they will get in the car and kill you. They are found at early levels in overrun military checkpoints and also around [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") and later military outposts. As mentioned in the Survival Guide, bullets hitting the head and chest have a small chance to ricochet and do no damage, so it is important to have a weapon that has a lot of damage and ammo to accommodate this.
