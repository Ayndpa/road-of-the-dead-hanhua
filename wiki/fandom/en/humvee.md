### Humvee
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Affiliation | Military |
| Type | AM General HMMWV |

The **AM General HMMWV** is a recurring vehicle in the Saga of the Dead.

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead")

The Humvee is one of the many static obstacles present in the game. In addition to that, [Soldiers](./enemy-soldier "Enemy Soldier") often hide behind them to shoot at [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman")'s [vehicle](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car") when he passes by.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2")

The Humvee reprises its role as obstacles, though it is also the primary playable vehicle, brought by [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") at the beginning.

The controllable Humvee is relatively sturdy, able to withstand a number of explosions and impacts, though it isn't the best at crew protection. The lack of a windshield weakens its matchup against zombies, but prevents splatters from obstructing the driver's vision and allows the passenger to simply shoot through the frame for defense. It can also be upgraded with [Mounted Weapons](./mounted-weapons "Mounted Weapons").
