## NORTHSIDE OF THE CITY

Northside had great parks, Fairview Mall, and good places before the outbreak. When the nightmare started, the worst happened here. First, zombies made their stand outside many major cities attacking people. Due to its virus like capabilities, their were mutations, causing mutants. The military was unable to stop the virus and a major epidemic erupted throughout the nation.

## SOUTHSIDE OF THE CITY

We play as John Creaseman trying to escape in the first game, on Southside Highway 65. This is the only key part we have about Southside but we do suggest that the Highway is pretty low as in the second game mutants can jump onto the highway. For a short time we do play Road of the Dead 2 on Highway 65.

## WESTSIDE OF THE CITY

In the second game you play as Cocheta Solomon and Diane Roseworth attempting to escape the city. You drive west in a desperate attempt and face much resistance along the way. This area has been completely destroyed and this area was covered in spike strips and mines to stop traffic.

## EASTSIDE OF THE CITY

Eastside is the opposite of Westside, and this is where Iache River is, as Cocheta remarks that Iache River is on the other side of the city. The military attempt to make a stand here, and however the rest is history, we have no idea whether or not the Iache River Stand was successful.
