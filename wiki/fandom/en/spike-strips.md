Spike Strips

 

The military and police have deployed numerous spike stripes on roads and highways around Evans Cty to enforce the quarantine. Civilians trying to escape in their vehicle don't get very far when they run over one of these, causing all their tires to go flat.

You can use your SWERVE ability to dodge these traps, explosions and rockets to blow thep up and upgrade tires to resist them better.
