**Hellfire** (referred to as **Hellfire-26** in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2")) is a recurring boss that appears in [Road of The Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead"), as an ally in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), and as a cameo in [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of the Dead").

## Gameplay

### [Road of The Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead")

After passing the fourth checkpoint on Burgony Street, Hellfire begins to intermittently spawn every few minutes to pursue the player (or after around 6km in Police State).

Hellfire's approach is usually telegraphed by him requesting permission to engage [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") over the radio, though certain sets of dialogue (like pre- or post-checkpoint chatter; [Bomber Jet](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") voice lines) can override this and cause him to silently spawn in front of the player.

Hellfire will follow the player as they change lanes and has two attacks:

- **Dual chainguns** that will tear through John like butter, but they do not deal any damage to his vehicle (other than obliterating the windshield if it's still intact). This is his most common attack, and has randomized length. Simply avoid being in front of him while he's firing, and you will not take damage.

- **Rockets** that will obliterate John and his vehicle, but they travel very slowly and are telegraphed by Hellfire sending a request to Headquarters. Additionally, Hellfire only begins using these after John reaches Northside Park in the campaign or around 11km in Police State. Swerve out of the way of the missile when it is fired to avoid damage.

Hellfire can be dispatched by luring him into colliding with three highway signs. Otherwise, it will automatically retreat once the player reaches Tunnel Drive.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

Hellfire returns in Road of the Dead 2, its challenge modes, and the level editor as both an enemy and an ally.

#### Campaign and Challenges

Hellfire will hover infront of the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") and fire its mounted weapons to destroy everything in its path with rockets and miniguns. Explosions from vehicles and Hellfire's rockets can cause friendly fire

In campaign-related modes, Hellfire only provides support for the entirity of the West Middletown segment.

In Mutant Madness, Out of Time and Dead State, Hellfire can be summoned manually and will remain for a limited duration before departing and entering cooldown. The chopper support perk increases its loiter time and recharge speed.

#### FUBAR

Hellfire returns as a recurring boss enemy in FUBAR, functioning identically to his counterpart in the first game.

With the same attacks, but a slightly slower following speed that makes it much easier to dodge his bullets, Hellfire is somewhat less of a threat. However, he should still not be underestimated, as 2 missile strikes even at maximum health and upgrades can kill you and health recovery requires med patches, which are few and far between.

There are three ways to dispatch him in FUBAR mode:

- Luring him into colliding with 3 highway signs (on Highway 65 maps)

- Attacking him with other weapons

- Luring him into the path of a carpet bombing

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")

Hellfire makes his first appearance after John enters Central City, being dispatched by Headquarters to take down [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") after he repeatedly manages to ram his way through the previous four checkpoints.

After his first engagement, Hellfire is bashed against the signs lining [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") and is forced into retreating back to base for an emergency landing. John continues on his path down the highway and has more close encounters with Hellfire, growing in intensity as time goes on and resulting in the pilot becoming more and more enraged.

By the time John reaches Northside Park, the [Military](./military "Military") is in full retreat and is no longer directly engaging him. Despite this, [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") orders Hellfire and the [air squadrons](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") to continue pursuing John and doing as much as they can. This involves giving Hellfire clearance to arm his air-to-ground missiles, a last ditch attempt to prevent John from escaping [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

Despite this, John manages to evade the missiles over their next few encounters, and passes through the derelict West Ridge. With the [destruction of the city](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") imminent, Hellfire pulls out and presumably evades the nuclear strike.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

By the time the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") make it to West Middletown, they encounter Hellfire passing above them. Successfully making contact, Hellfire warns them that they need to regroup with the other [Military](./military "Military") units at the Iachi River, and offers to covering fire so that the duo can reach [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") to catch a transport.

Hellfire provides air support for the duo until they reach the Madera District, obliterating almost everything standing between the trio and salvation. Once they've arrived, Hellfire reminds them to hurry to [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") before the transports depart for the river, and then heads over there himself to engage [a civilian on the run](./john-creasman "John Creasman").

The duo, now passing through Chinatown, angrily contact Hellfire over the radio to question as to where the transports are. Hellfire, realizing their situation, warns the duo that they're too late and the transports have left without them. Unable to help further, Hellfire tells the duo 'good luck, stay alive'.

Despite Cocheta's later attempts to contact Hellfire-26 over the radio, the vehicle and its pilot are never seen or heard from again.

### [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of the Dead")

Hellfire makes a brief cameo in the ending cutscene of [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of the Dead"), seen flying 'a little west of Senoia', implied to be the town of Alliance.

## Trivia

- Hellfire is based on the [AH-64 Apache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_AH-64_Apache "wikipedia:Boeing AH-64 Apache") attack helicopter, and named after the [AGM-114 Hellfire Missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AGM-114_Hellfire "wikipedia:AGM-114 Hellfire") compatible with it. The in-game Hellfire is equipped with two [Hydra 70](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydra_70 "wikipedia:Hydra 70") rocket pods and two pylon-mounted M230 Chain Guns, rather than the singular nose gun seen on real Apaches.

- Hellfire's rivalry with [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") is likely a nod to the [2004 Killdozer incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvin_Heemeyer "wikipedia:Marvin Heemeyer"), as one of the proposed ways to take down Heemeyer's bulldozer involved using an Apache armed with Hellfire missiles.

- Judging by the fact that Hellfire never outright crashes during the events of either game, it is heavily implied that the Hellfire seen in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") is the same one seen in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"), and that all Hellfires in the latter are the same helicopter.

- Surprisingly, Hellfire is able to keep up with [John's Mustang](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car") even while flying backwards at approximately 150mph (240kmh).
