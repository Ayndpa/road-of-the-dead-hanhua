Senoia is a town mentioned in [*Road Of The Dead 2*](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2") and *[Lab Of The Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead")*. According to reports from Caspers unit, the town is overrun and infested with undead. Also, according to [Allen C. Tyler](./allen-c-tyler "Allen C. Tyler"), the town is located somewhere in the desert.
