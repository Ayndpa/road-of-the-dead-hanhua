Fuel Trucks

 

Many fuel trucks have been abandoned in various places in Evans City. There can be a lot of them industrial sections especially. These vehicles carry relative fuel that can be ignited, causing a huge explosion with just a few shots from any weapon or nearby explosions.

The large area of effect of its explosion can take out many other objects and vehicles, causing chain reactions. Crashing into one of these trucks can be fatal.
