Machine gun turret can only be purchased with RP at Weapon Caches, Price is 1250 RP.

You can only have ammo for weapon installed at a time,if you want extra ammo then you have to buy ammo at Weapons Caches for 50 RP.

 ![Machine Gun Turret](/fandom-img/Machine_Gun_Turret.png) 

 

This *Machine gun turret(Mounted MG)* fires powerful rounds that will do massive damage on everything. Watch the heat meter or it will overheat and stop functioning until it cools off.
Can *Machine gun turret* to blow up, althrough the mounted machinegun takes a few shots. The machine gun turret can explode, it only takes a few shots to explode the target or shoot dead zombies within 1 bullet

Ammunition for these mounted weapons cannot be replenished and therefore need to be repurchasede when they run out of ammo.
