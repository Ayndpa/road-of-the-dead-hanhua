Weapon Caches

 

During the events of [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2"), Weapon caches can be found in every part of [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"), left behind by military personnel. These caches contain [new](./diane-s-weapons "Diane's Weapons") [weapons](./cocheta-s-weapons "Cocheta's Weapons") , [vehicle weapon attachments](./mounted-weapons "Mounted Weapons") and full ammo replenishments. Note that weapon caches are the only locations with [vehicle](./humvee "Humvee") turret attachments.

Individual weapon and weapon upgrades can also be obtained, all at the cost of RP.
