Feeders

 

The undead have an insatiable appetite, devouring everyone they can reach. Usually they will eat a small portion of a victim and then lose interest as the meat turns cold and starts to reanimate into another undead.

Sometimes however, they enter into a feeding frenzy, stuffing themselves with as much of a body as they can fit inside their stomach, often causing their internal organs to burst and leak whatever they are eating back to the ground.

[Common](./common-undead "Common Undead") and [Ravager](./ravager "Ravager") undead can often be seen in a feeding frenzy. They will ignore other living people around them, unless shot at or provoked, then they will stand up and come after you. Running over a feeder will grind lots of gore into your wheels, temporarily slowing you down quite a lot.
