Churches

 

Churches can be found throughout [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"). During the initial quarantine, the military designated churches as chopper rescue points for evacuating uninfected [civilians](./civilians "Civilians") and personnel. This was soon scrapped as the quarantine was fully enforced, favoring to try to keep the epidemic contained. These locations still have fortified doors and food rations within.

Rescued civilians can be dropped off at these locations for RP bonuses.

Medical shots can also be purchased for RP at churches.
