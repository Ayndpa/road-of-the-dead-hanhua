Police Stations

 

Police stations can be found throughout [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"). During the initial quarantine, the military designated police stations as a location for chopper rescue points for evacuating uninfected [civilians](./civilians "Civilians") and personnel. This was soon scrapped as the quarantine was fully enforced, favoring to try to keep the epidemic contained. These locations still have fortified doors and food rations within.

Weapons and weapon upgrades can be purchased with RP in police stations.

Rescued civilians can be dropped off at these locations for RP bonuses as well.
