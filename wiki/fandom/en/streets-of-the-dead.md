***Streets of the Dead*** is an upcoming game for Saga of the Dead, announced by [Evil-Dog Games](./evil-dog "Evil-dog") on January 29, 2017 as a mass production for his Twitch stream. It is implied that [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") will make a return for this game.
