Security Fences

 

Military security checkpoints fences can be found at quarantine checkpoints along streets and highways. They can also be found in alleyways, in failed attempts to wall off the undead hordes.

Running over these fences does small to no damage to [your vehicle](./humvee "Humvee"). When the zombie passes through the fence, it will be stuck for a short time and then the fence will collapse.

When shooting at targets behind a fence, there is a small chance the bullets will strike the fence itself instead of the target.
