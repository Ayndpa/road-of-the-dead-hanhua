Various **gamemodes** are available in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), ranging from the story mode to challenge modes and time trials.

## [Road of The Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead")

[Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") has four gamemodes. All are available from the start of the game.

### The Great Escape

*"Speed through the zombie apocalypse and escape the quarantined city! Normal difficulty."*

The story mode. [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), a mechanic, becomes trapped inside [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") as the zombie apocalypse begins and decides to escape despite the brutal military crackdown on people attempting to leave. As the tagline suggests, this is the normal difficulty and the one most friendly to new players: the car takes less damage, [soldiers](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") and [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire") inflict less damage, and there are less zombies and vehicles.

### Highway to Hell

*"This is the road for survivors with something to prove! Hard difficulty."*

The hardcore version of the story mode with identical plot and chapters. Unlike *The Great Escape*, in this mode all damage is increased: the windshield takes incredibly low amounts of damage before shattering even at maximum upgrade level, the car's bumper and engine are significantly weaker, and John takes extremely high damage from both soldiers and Hellfire. Additionally, debris and vehicle spawns along with zombie counts are drastically increased.

### Dead on Time

*"You're living on borrowed time. Get as far as you can by killing zombies to earn extra time!"*

The time trial mode. A 30-second timer starts once you begin and is increased by hitting zombies with your car (adding 2 or 3 seconds randomly) along with feeders. Civilians deduct 10 seconds from the timer. Soldiers do not increase or decrease the timer. Military spawns are greatly decreased and take much longer to intensify compared to the other gamemodes. If the timer hits 0, the car will instantly explode and fail the player.

### Police State

*"This is war and you are a one man army on wheels! Get as far as you can while facing brutal military resistance!"*

The endless mode. Military spawns are greatly increased along with Hellfire's escalation rate, making him much more of a threat as the game goes on. Otherwise, difficulty is identical to *The Great Escape*: soldiers and Hellfire do lower damage, the car is more robust, and the windshield can take more hits. Checkpoints are now randomized instead of being in set locations due to the lack of chapters in this mode.

## [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2")

[Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") has seven gamemodes. *Lost Guns*, *Out of Time*, and *Dead State* are available from the start, while the remaining four must be unlocked by completing *Lost Guns*.

### Lost Guns

*"Play as two soldiers trying to escape the quarantined city while the military operation fails to contain the outbreak."*

The story mode. [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon"), two soldiers left inside [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") during the same time as [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"), decide to try and escape the city as the military operation meant to contain the infection has completely failed. The normal difficulty. [Chopper Support](./hellfire "Hellfire") is not available in this mode outside of scripted sequences.

### Hardcore Mode

*"In hardcore mode, you must complete the story without ever dying. Are you truly able to survive the zombie apocalypse?"*

The hardcore version of the story mode. Unlike *Highway to Hell* in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"), there are no additional increases in difficulty or vehicle clutter. The player's progress is not saved, and will be reset back to the first stage if they lose or quit. [Chopper Support](./hellfire "Hellfire") is not available in this mode outside of scripted sequences.

### Out of Time

*"Race against the clock in this intense game mode where killing zombies give you extra time to survive."*

The time trial mode, based on the version from [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"). Functions identically: a 30-second timer starts upon starting, killing zombies gives you a random amount of additional time between 1-5 seconds, and both zombie and environmental hazard escalation levels are decreased. [Chopper Support](./hellfire "Hellfire") is available in this mode.

### Dead State

*"Go as far as you can in this survival mode, battling the zombie hordes and driving through dangerous areas."*

An endless survival mode with no military intervention. [Chopper Support](./hellfire "Hellfire") is available in this mode.

### Rescue Op

*"Play as the gunner on a rescue chopper and rescue civilians from their sieged buildings."*

An endless mode similar to the final segment of Lost Guns where the player defends their helicopter and a civilian-occupied facility from zombies. Zombies who manage to break inside will vanish, taking some civilians with them. Once all of the zombies have been slain, any surviving civilians will enter the helicopter. The game ends only if a zombie gets too close to the helicopter or if all civilians at a single location perish.

### Mutant Meltdown

*"Face the wrath of the mutants in this insane challenge mode."*

A variant of *Dead State*, however this one has the unique trait of having an alternate spawnlist consisting only of [Mutant Zombies](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") in great numbers, [Civilians](./civilians "Civilians"), and [Grubs](./grub "Grub"). Otherwise, basically identical to the other gamemodes minus having no zombies. [Chopper Support](./hellfire "Hellfire") is available in this mode.

### [F.U.B.A.R.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_military_slang_terms#FUBAR "wikipedia:List of military slang terms")

*"You're going rogue as the military operation is doomed to fail. As deserters, you become the enemy."*

A revival of the *Police State* gamemode from [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"), featuring the original game's soldiers with updated mechanics to account for suppression and the new zombie types along with the original game's voicelines for [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire") and the [Bomber Jets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet"). Hellfire is hostile in this mode and functions identically to how he did in the original game. Of course, zombies are also present in great numbers. [Chopper Support](./hellfire "Hellfire") is not available in this mode for obvious reasons.
