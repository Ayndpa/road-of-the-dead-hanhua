**Evans City** was a major population center in which the campaigns of both [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") take place, but it is also mentioned in [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead") and [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of the Dead").

## Overview

**Evans City** is a large city and presumably a major population center, housing millions of people. It comprises mostly of commercial districts, but also includes several residential and industrial districts along with multiple parks and forests. Additionally, Evans City is densely populated with auto-shops, police stations, and churches.

Due to the panic and chaos of the quarantine and outbreak, the streets and highways are mostly covered in wrecked vehicles, the undead, and other hazards such as landmines and spikestrips by the time both games take place. Many areas also have thick fog, notably the early sections of [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") in Road of the Dead and Candor Hill in Road of the Dead 2.

Presumably, the Iache River is located near the city, as it is later used as the [military](./military "Military")'s primary evacuation site. It is also located near a desert, judging by the environments seen at the end of Road of the Dead and Road of the Dead 2.

#### **[Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")**

  - **Red Ridge:** Checkpoint 3 tries and fails to stop John here, but manages to send his front plate data to Headquarters while he passes.

  - **Central City:** The site of the first dispatch of [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire") to the Highway. Checkpoint 5 tries and fails to stop Creasman here, and is overwhelmed by zombies immediately afterwards.
  - **Down Town:** The first instances of [bomb squads](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") being dispatched to blow up Creasman's [vehicle](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car"). Checkpoint 6 makes their stand against Creasman here, but ultimately fails as well.
  - **Chinatown:** Similar to Downtown. Mutants begin to appear sporadically here in low numbers. Checkpoint 7 is ordered to stand down and let John through, failing to stop his progress.
  - **Brighton Square:** The site of the first [bombing runs](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") called in against John. Checkpoint 8 makes the last sanctioned attempt to stop John Creasman at the end of the district.
  - **Northside Park:** A park area. The site of the [Military](./military "Military") evacuations on Highway 65. Checkpoint 9, a self-sacrificial garrison ignoring their evacuation orders, makes the Military's final attempt to stop John Creasman.
  - **West Ridge:** Located near the western end of Evans City. Has a derelict military checkpoint and high mutant population.
  - **Tunnel Drive:** The final stretch leading to the desert outside the city. High density of mutants and abandoned vehicles.

## Timeline

### Beginning of the Outbreak

In [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), the game's loading screen gives us a timeline and some further insight regarding the week leading up to the events of [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2").

- On **Day One** of the outbreak, isolated events of an unidentified sickness and several violent incidents are reported in around Evans City.

- On **Day Two** of the outbreak, the news reports on a man dying of the unidentified illness. After being rushed to the hospital, bloody violence and chaos ensued in its aftermath.

- On **Day Three**, more reports of sick and seemingly dead bodies committing random acts of violence come in. Some witnesses state that they have seen acts of cannibalism as well, but the more concerning and grisly details are withheld to prevent panic.

- On **Day Four**, the [CDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention "wikipedia:Centers for Disease Control and Prevention") moves into Evans City. They set up makeshift safe zones to try and contain the unknown illness, and further research reveals that bites and deep scratches are the main methods of the sickness transmission. Those infected are clinically dead, but still move and attack violently, now forming mobs.

- On **Day Five**, a media blackout is set into place and all news channels are forced off the air. [Military](./military "Military") personnel move into Evans City and institute a city-wide quarantine. No one is permitted to enter the city, and those attempting to leave will be shot on sight. Officials broadcast the rules of the quarantine over the radio: keep calm, stay indoors, and remain out of sight until further notice.
  - [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), overhearing this news and seeing his neighbors be attacked by the undead, begins to prepare his escape.
  - Outside news channels report that there has been a chemical leak and that it is currently being cleaned up, with government officials preventing the truth of the matter from being released to the public.

- On **Day Six**, riots and panic break out as people attempt to leave **Evans City**. Hundreds are arrested and put into makeshift internment camps at local schools and jails by [military personnel](./military-soldier "Military Soldier"). The [Military](./military "Military") and [CDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention "wikipedia:Centers for Disease Control and Prevention") set up quarantine checkpoints throughout **Evans City** in an effort to uphold the quarantine and prevent people from leaving. Despite this, more get infected and lash out violently. Thousands are killed.
  - [Churches](./churches "Churches") and [Police Stations](./police-stations "Police Stations") are set up as last minute evacuation zones.
  - [Military personnel](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") receive new orders: shoot anyone approaching checkpoints.
  - [CDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention "wikipedia:Centers for Disease Control and Prevention") personnel begin burning bodies.

### [Day](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") [Seven](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

The [infected walking corpses](./common-undead "Common Undead") start to outnumber living people remaining in the city. Checkpoints are being rapidly overrun, with Dispatch stating that the rate is approximately '12 forward posts in just 10 minutes'.

A central command and communications checkpoint is overrun by a panicked riot of hundreds of civilians, followed by a horde of the undead. In the chaos, a high security broadcast radio is stolen. Communication breaks down throughout all military and government channels as the military forces left in the city are quickly thrown into disarray.

#### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead")

[John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), completing his final preparations, drives his [armored Mustang](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car") out of his garage and onto [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65"). Quickly alerting [Military](./military "Military") officials to his escape attempt, almost all of the resources in the city are devoted to bombarding [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") and preventing John from escaping the city.

By the time he reaches Central City, the [Military](./military "Military") is being overrun at an alarming rate, and John finds out that his rampage is causing the checkpoints he passes through to immediately be swarmed and overwhelmed by the undead. With the military now expiring [air](./hellfire "Hellfire") [assets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") in a desperate final few attempts to prevent him from leaving, [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") is completely overrun.

The remaining personnel on [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") are ordered by [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") to abandon their effort to stop Creasman, and instead evacuate to the Iache River to escape the city. Sherman quickly gets approval to drop a nuke on the city, and [Phantom Eagle](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") arms the nuclear warhead and prepares to drop it right as John escapes through the tunnel at the end of the city.

#### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

[Kilo-5](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth"), a [military patrol](./military-soldier "Military Soldier") completing their orders, crashes and is overturned outside a residential area. With the lone survivor, [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth"), desperately making a final stand against a mob of the undead, a second soldier named [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") arrives and kills the remaining undead.

The duo enter the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") and begin heading west towards the edge of the city, with Cocheta commenting that the [Military](./military "Military") is losing control of the city at an alarming rate and that they have no options but to stay in small numbers and ignore their current orders to go north to regroup with the other units.

Despite [jets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") being called in extremely close to them as the [Military](./military "Military") continues their last-ditch attempt to contain the outbreak, the duo pass through an industrial district and are assisted by [Hellfire-26](./hellfire "Hellfire") in making it to [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") to try and find a transport to take them out of the city.

Unfortunately, it's too late. Outside of encountering a [black armored Mustang](./john-creasman "John Creasman") overtaking them on the highway over Downtown, the Highway is already overrun and all transports have departed. Soon after, the duo overhear [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") giving [Phantom Eagle](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") the orders to drop the nuke on **Evans City** to contain it once and for all.

Now with no more allies to help them, the duo get off [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") at Sylmar Avenue and begin heading east. With HQ putting them on hold, the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") begin to give up hope. Reaching Northside Park, the duo finally manage to contact [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") and begin heading towards the Fairview Mall to meet up with him and escape the city before the nuke is dropped.

Successfully reaching the mall and defending Sherman's helicopter from the zombies whilst it gets repaired, the duo narrowly escape the city seconds before the nuke is dropped.

### Aftermath

The nuclear warhead impacts with **Evans City**, destroying colossal swaths of it in the ensuing explosion.

Unfortunately, the zombies have managed to spread too far, and the nuclear weapon is unable to eliminate all of the undead (particularly the [mutated ones](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies"). With the remaining [Military](./military "Military") forces on ground in the city eliminated, including all of the Transport Units that had been making their way out through the Iache River, the operation is ultimately a failure.

Headquarters is quickly overrun afterward, dealing a severe blow to the remaining government personnel by shattering the chain of command and cutting off most of the [Military](./military "Military")'s remaining communication in the state of Pennsylvania whilst other cities are overrun aswell.

[General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman"), discovering the situation for himself through the radio transmissions ensuing the nuclear blast, begins to make preparations to travel to the nearby town of Alliance to fortify and defend it from the undead mob along with a unit named **Casper One**. [Diane](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon"), who are still accompanying him, agree to the plan.

[John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), watching the city be wiped away by the nuclear blast, silently re-enters his car and drives into the desert.

## Trivia

- The town references the real [Evans City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evans_City,_Pennsylvania) where the zombie movie [Night of the Living Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_of_the_Living_Dead) was shot, though it's much larger than its namesake.
