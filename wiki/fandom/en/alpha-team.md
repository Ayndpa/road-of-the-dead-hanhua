**Alpha Team** is the name given by [Allen C. Tyler](./allen-c-tyler "Allen C. Tyler") to refer to the previous occupants of the abandoned underground facility near [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") in [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead"). The group's true name is unknown, their objective however is slowly revealed throughout the game through [tapes](./alpha-tapes "Alpha Tapes") and [notes](./alpha-notes "Alpha Notes") that the player can research and read to find out what happened to the previous occupants.

Alpha Team was in charge of Operation Flatline, a military operation designed to create the [S14 bioweapon](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus"). After the virus's completion, the team then moved onto creating the S15 virus designed as a failsafe for the S14 weapon in case of a disaster. Dr Lori Benedict was assigned on the operation to lead the anti-virus's development. She was employed under the guise of fixing a mood control virus instead of developing a bioweapon failsafe. Alpha Team's members were required to give a monthly(?) log of their experience which was to be reviewed by Dr Lori and the other superiors,

Staff of Alpha Team showed a complete disregard for proper security protocols, often going close to specimen infected with the S14 virus to essentially bully them. Dr Lori showed strong resent to this but was unable to really do anything to prevent this.

As time went on, Lori eventually realises the truth of the S14 virus but relunctantly continues the project, not sure what else to do.

Private Peter Johnson was a guard assigned to patrolling the specimen storage area. During a regular patrol, Pvt. Peter got too close to a infected specimen and was scratched, the virus had entered his bloodstream. Peter tried to hide the fact, reporting in his log that it was a minor cut and that it was no big deal. Lori on the other hand did not. While reviewing the logs, Lori realised that the scratch could have been a carrier for the virus and that Peter could possibly be infected and inadvertably release the virus. She demanded a facility wide search for Peter but it was too late. By the time Peter was found and shot on sight, most of the North Wing of the facility staff was lost to infected.

Admist the chaos, Lori decides the best course of action is to make sure the virus can never be reproduced and destroys all research on the genetic materials of the virus, essentially making it impossible to reproduce in a lab. Captain Walker, the military superior in charge, treats this act as treason against the state and executes Lori before dealing, and failing to push back the zombies. The zombies escape to the surface, manage to find their way to [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") and start the [Saga of The Dead](./saga-of-the-dead-wiki "Saga of The Dead Wiki").
