## Biography

Johnny is a professional Rambo impersonator and Segway enthusiast; he is the protagonist of Segway of the Dead, racing through Evans City trying to save everyone from the zombies.

## Appearances

### Segway of the Dead

Following the recent surge in ghost sightings, Johnny has settled into a spacious garage that, oddly enough, bears a striking resemblance to the local mechanic and fugitive John Creasman. Johnny, along with his trusty ride, a lightweight electric bicycle commonly known as a Segway, sits motionless, likely contemplating his glorious escape.

## Trivia

- - This is the only protagonist who dies in the saga, despite being from an April Fool's Day game.
  - In Road of the Dead 2 main menu, you can see the (probably) same zombie horde Johnny tried breaking through, walking towards the city.
