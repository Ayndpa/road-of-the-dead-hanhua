Meds Shop

 

Medical shots can be purchased with RP at Churches. You can purchase the shots up to 5 times each.

The steroid shot will grant both characters a temporary personal resistance to damage, allowing them to go through explosions unscathed.

Med Patch shots are a cocktail of many things that will alleaviate your characters' suffering (indicatated by the screen flashing red) and restore their health.
