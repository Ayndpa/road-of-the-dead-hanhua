***Road of The Dead 2*** is the long anticipated sequel to [*Road of The Dead*](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead") developed by [Evil-Dog Games](./evildog-games "EvilDog Games") and is a more tactic orientated (genre) game compared to its fast paced predecessor.

*Road of The Dead 2* is set concurrently with *Road of The Dead* and the fall of Evan City. The game focuses on 2 soldiers, [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon"), trying to escape a rapidly worsening [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") in their Humvee before the zombies completely overrun the city, similar to the plot of its predecessor but with the perspective of the soldiers.

This time around, there is a variety of firearms to shoot zombies from a distance. Either character can be chosen for the driver or gunner. Firearms can be upgraded either with the in-game's currency RP and through experience. See [Upgrades](./road-of-the-dead-2-upgrades "Road of the Dead 2/Upgrades").

## Plot

The opening cutscene shows an overturned military Humvee with a still-living soldier under one end. Almost immediately, with much screaming, the soldier is pulled into the car (assuming the back window is completely shattered) and devoured by a hidden zombie, with blood pouring out from the area he was last seen at.

Then Diane is shown barely fending off a [Brute](./brutes "Brutes") and succeeds in killing it by acquiring a nearby pistol and shooting it three times in the head. She is quickly surrounded by a Common Undead, a Ravager and a Brute who heard their scuffle, but is saved when another Humvee smashes into the zombies. The driver comes out and finishes a split-in-half Brute with his pistol. The driver, Cocheta, offers help to Diane and they drive away in Cocheta's Humvee, with the plan to contact and regroup with other military personnel.

While driving through the zombie-infested streets, they hear on their radio that the city-wide quarantine is failing, with military checkpoints across the city being overrun one by one.

They contact HQ, to get a clear picture. HQ advises them to head northern side of the city to regroup and evacuate with the remaining personnel.

Diane and Cocheta decide against that, heading west to the edge of the city, because they suddenly lost contact with HQ.

After passing through zombie-filled neighborhoods, abandoned military outposts, and evading carpet bombings by war planes (Due to communications being thrown into disarray, these fighter jets were simply ordered to bomb everything, in hopes of killing the infected).

Thanks to their radio, they make contact with a helicopter which is headed for the north. The pilot, after questioning Diane and Cocheta's motives, provides aerial support for a little while, allowing Diane and Cocheta to replenish ammo if needed. The pilot advises the two soldiers to head to Chinatown, where a military convoy is currently stationed.

After the helicopter leaves, Diane and Cocheta make it onto the highway where [John](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), the main character who escaped from the first game, is seen blazing past the military vehicle, shouting: "Yeehaw! Smile, baby!". However, they learn that the Chinatown convoy has already left - they arrived too late. Then on the radio, Diane and Cocheta overhear military personnel announcing an impending nuclear strike in a last ditch effort to wipe out the zombies. Cocheta panics, aware their vehicle cannot hope to outrun the blast. He quickly decides to leave the highway in a desperate search for a transport.

After several fruitless attempts, the duo reach Flyboy 1, General Sherman's helicopter. Sherman initially tries to dismiss them, claiming rescue will arrive in thirty minutes. Cocheta angrily berates Sherman for authorizing the nuclear strike. Sherman stands by his position and still refuses, believing their request impractical given their distance. Cocheta then suggests both parties to converge at Fairview Mall and derides Sherman for writing off his forces. Sherman relents, and warns them the helicopter will not wait forever.

Cocheta and Diane were slightly behind schedule, but manage to reach Flyboy 1. However, a mechanical fault prevents it from taking off immediately. The pilot, Baliski, offers to fix it in five minutes, but Sherman allows only two, so they have time to clear the blast. Diane and Cocheta successfully defend it until takeoff, and it manages to evade the detonation.

When the helicopter and the people inside recover from the shock wave of the nuke, General Sherman contacts the [Phantom Eagle](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") pilot for a situation report once the radiation is clear. It turns out Sherman's decision backfired completely, with many zombies still at large. A furious Cocheta tries to attack Sherman for the senseless deaths but is quickly restrained by Sherman's own forces.

Sherman then tries to contact several units at other cities and the HQ, all of which either did not respond or were on the verge of being overrun. The HQ fell shortly after, and while unit Caspar answers, they have taken heavy losses and are hoping for a retreat, which he authorizes. He then reveals the military was quickly overwhelmed by many simultaneous outbreaks, hindered by miscommunication and lack of intel.

Out of options, Sherman plans to regroup with anyone he can still reach at Alliance, a more defensible location. Everyone on Flyboy 1 agree, as is unit Caspar. He then swears revenge, declaring they are not quite done.

## Upgrades

*Main article: [Upgrades (RotD 2)](./road-of-the-dead-2-upgrades "Road of the Dead 2/Upgrades")*

In between the runs you can buy new things to upgrade your car, weapons, skills and meds. Those upgrades are important for your success.

The upgrades are separated into 6 sections: Vehicle, Meds, Perks, Weapons (Cocheta), Weapons (Diane) and Mounted Weapons.

**VEHICLE:** In this section you can Upgrade the Tires, Engine and Armor.

TIRES: It affects how fast you can steer, how fast you can brake and how you handle spike strips and oil spills.

ENGINE: Increases the engine's HP and your acceleration.

ARMOR: Increases the amount of collision you can take before exploding.

**MEDS:** In this section you can buy things that restore HP of your characters.

MED PATCH: It heals any damage you took, so using it at the right moment is crucial.

STEROIDS: Those things will make you Immune to bullets or explosions for a moment.

**[PERKS](./perks "Perks"):** This sections have upgrades that benefit you across the board.

CHOPPER ASSIST: Unlocks chopper assist which will occasionally let you call in Hellfire 26 for air support much like in the campaign, however is not available in Story mode, FUBAR or Rescue Op. Further upgrading increases the extent of air support before disengaging.

MORE AMMO: Increases the amount of ammo gathered at ammo points such as military vehicles

LOWER COSTS: Lowers the cost of all purchasables in the game down by 50% at max level

FASTER SWITCH: Increases the speed that your characters swaps positions

FASTER TASKS: Increases the speed at which tasks are performed

FASTER LEVELING: Provides faster leveling of all weapons, driving and toughness
