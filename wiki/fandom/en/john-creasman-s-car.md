### John Creasman's Car
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Affiliation | John Creasman |
| Type | Ford Mustang GT (fifth-generation) |

**John Creasman's Car** is a recurring and drivable vehicle in Saga of the Dead, owned by [John Creaman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") himself.

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead")

John Creasman's Car is the main and only form of transport in Road of the Dead. He drives a 2005 fifth-generation Ford Mustang GT. In its stock form, the car can reach a top speed of 160 km/h (around 100 mph) and accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in roughly ten seconds.

The Mustang features a gray racing stripe running across the hood and roof, tinted windows, and a red interior. Inside, it has two seats, a speedometer, air-conditioning vents positioned at the top of the dashboard, a fuel gauge, a manual transmission, and a radio. In [Police State](./gamemodes#Police_State "Gamemodes") and [Dead on Time](./gamemodes#Dead_on_Time "Gamemodes"), there is a kilometer counter under the speedometer. The interior shows visible wear, with several cracked and scratched components.

The car’s appearance changes each time it crashes into vehicles. Running over civilians, zombies, or soldiers causes blood to splatter across the windshield, which can be cleared using the wipers. If the windshield is broken, however, the blood spreads into the interior and onto John’s arm, where it cannot be cleaned.

John’s Mustang also becomes playable in Road of the Dead 2 after the final credits. Functionally, it behaves almost the same as the Humvee, although the speedometer does not work, and the side mirrors are missing.

Certain upgrades have trade-offs. For example, the Level 3 bumper significantly increases damage output but adds weight to the car, making it harder to turn.

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2")

At the beginning of the Downtown segment, John Creasman’s car speeds past the screen as he shouts, “Yee-haw! Smile, baby!” During this sequence, The vehicle is not a solid entity, as it is completely invulnerable, cannot set off mines, and can pass through obstacles. The car later reappears in a post-credits scene, where John is shown driving away.
The vehicle is also playable, though it is only unlocked after completing [Lost Guns](./gamemodes#Lost_Guns "Gamemodes") and watching the credits. It functions almost identically to the Humvee. However, [Evil-Dog](./evil-dog "Evil-dog") stated that John Creasman’s car has a higher top speed at the cost of lower health and slower braking. The top-down cutscenes used for highway transitions are adjusted to match the vehicle. Unlike its Road of the Dead counterpart, the dashboard is non-functional.

## Trivia

- In the garage and during the final cutscene, the Mustang resembles a 2005–2009/fifth-generation Ford Mustang GT. When the car is destroyed in-game, however, the rear design more closely matches the 2010 model.
- Despite lacking turn signals, normally located inside the headlights, the car’s overall design appears to be primarily based on the 2010 Mustang.
- There is a minor voice-line error related to the Mustang. [Military personnel](./military-units "Military Units") mention identifying the driver by the front license plate, despite the car not shown to have one.
