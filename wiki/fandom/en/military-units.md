### Battle Situation:

As it stands, the US military and National Guard have been largely thrown into disarray and disorganization due to the lack of information on how the undead operates and how to properly combat them, the ineptitude of military leadership leading to many units being overrun, and the widespread chaos now ensuing across the nation. As stated by General Sherman, the military has been deployed into a plethora of areas and cities nationwide and is currently engaging the growing numbers of undead, but its forces have been spread too thin, leading to many defenseless and poorly-reinforced regions being overrun. It can also be assumed that many of the major urban areas the military have been deployed into are meeting a fate similar to Evans City, with many streets and neighborhoods overrun and thrown into anarchy with the military losing complete control and leadership refusing to acknowledge the situation, or a fractions of what started as a containment area are left.

It is unknown how many troops were in Evans City before the nuke was launched, but there are at least 40 confirmed units, most likely platoon size, and Cocheta mentions entire regiments were massacred, meaning there was at least a brigade element sent to Evans City. This also means that General Sherman may have been a Brigadier General since he is the only general present in both games.

In addition to Army units, there were Air Force and Army Aviation elements present, as seen with the Hellfire unit and the fighter jets. Oddly, however, there were no presence of armored units, but only motorized regiments with only basic motorized equipment such as Humvees and LMTVs. This may imply that the [National Guard Bureau](./military "Military") might have only focused on more important cities such as New York city, Los Angeles, Denver, Miami, Seattle, San-Francisco etc rather than a small city.
