Water Barrels

 

Barrels filled with water, can be found on the highway, and in some parts of the [city](./evans-city "Evans City"). Used in case a car loses control at high speeds, will hit the barrels instead of concrete walls.

Run over these barrels to spill water onto your engine and extinghishing any fire that may be engulfing it. This will prevent your vehicle from exploding on a further collision. It will also cool down a smoking engine.
