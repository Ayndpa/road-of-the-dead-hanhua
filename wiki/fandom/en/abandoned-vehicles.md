Abandoned vehicles litter the streets and all throughout [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"). Some crashed, some destroyed and others abandoned by panicked civilians.

There are 3 common sizes: small, medium and large vehicles. The larger the size, the more damage it may cause to your vehicle if you crash into them.

These vehicles can be blown up with gun fire and much easier with the rocket launcher, mounted MG or other means of explosions, resulting in chain explosions.
