**John Creasman** is the protagonist of [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead") and widely considered to be the overall main protagonist of the [Saga of the Dead](./saga-of-the-dead-wiki "Saga of The Dead Wiki") series, despite only being the protagonist in one game. However, he is commonly referred to in the [other](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") [games](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead") and it is implied that he will appear in future Saga of the Dead games such as [Evil-Dog](./evil-dog "Evil-dog")'s planned project, [Streets of the Dead](./streets-of-the-dead "Streets of the Dead").

## Biography

John Creasman was a mechanic living in [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") before the [zombie virus](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus") broke loose. According to a conversation between Headquarters and Dispatch, John has a prior record of disorderly conduct. Judging by the emphasis on upgrades, John is likely highly skilled at his profession, and has some degree of firearms experience.

## Appearances

### Road of the Dead

John, aged 36 years old at the time, is working in his garage on a [car](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car") on day 5 of the outbreak. When the rock music on the radio is suddenly interrupted by an emergency broadcast ordering everyone to stay indoors, John witnesses his neighbors being attacked by [zombies](./common-undead "Common Undead") outside. Tuning his radio to a military frequency, John decides he needs to escape the city.

Upgrading his car over the following two days, he envisions what might happen to him once he gets on the highway and plans his escape accordingly, accounting for [jets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet"), [attack helicopters](./hellfire "Hellfire"), [soldiers](./enemy-soldier "Enemy Soldier"), and even [mutants](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") in addition to the undead.

On the seventh day of the outbreak, John enters [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65") and is almost immediately noticed by the [Military](./military "Military") after approaching the military checkpoint at Monroeville. Repeatedly managing to evade soldiers stationed on the ground and even spikestrips deployed to blow out his tires, he reaches Central City and immediately has [an attack helicopter](./hellfire "Hellfire") dispatched to take him down. John causes the chopper to retreat, now causing checkpoints to be overrun as he bashes through them.

By the time [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman") takes over, John has reached the sixth military checkpoint at Chinatown. Progressing further, even when [mutants](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") and [fighter jets](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") are sent to take him down, John repeatedly defies the final attempts by the [Military](./military "Military") to take him down. Once they finally give up on him after he enters the West Ridge, John passes through an abandoned military checkpoint whilst overhearing news that [a jet](./phantom-eagle "Phantom Eagle") is about to drop a nuke on [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

Entering the tunnel just in time to avoid being nuked, John watches Evans City get obliterated by the nuke, only to hear the [Military](./military "Military") panicking about how the nuke has failed to contain the infection.

### Road of the Dead 2

John makes a cameo during [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") as he passes through the Downtown district, overtaking [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") as they travel down [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65"). The duo comment on how John is crazy, but whatever he's doing must be working because he's still alive.

John appears again in the postcredits scene, mirroring the nuke scene from the first game as he watches it detonate. Pulling off his welding mask, John climbs back into his blood-soaked and damaged car and drives off into the desert with three [mutants](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") in hot pursuit of him.

## Trivia

- [Allen C. Tyler](./allen-c-tyler "Allen C. Tyler") from [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead") reveals his middle name to be Creasman in his final log, and speaks of his brother in Evan City in a previous log, implying John to be his brother. Also interestingly, Allen speaks of John in a negative manner before realizing his possible demise suggesting a falling out.
  - Since Tyler makes no indication that he and John are anything less than full-blooded siblings, the odd naming convention of Tyler's full name could be explained by their parents separating, with the mother taking custody of Tyler and making him use either her maiden name or the last name of her new partner.
  - According to promotional material from Evil-Dog for the release of Road of the Dead 2, John is 36 years old, making him the older brother.

- After completing the main campaign of [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), John's [Ford Mustang](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car") becomes available for [Diane](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") and [Cocheta](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") to drive as an alternative to the Humvee.
  - Unfortunately, it functions identically to the [Humvee](./humvee "Humvee") minus some minor aesthetic changes.

- Oddly, John's hair changes from black to brown between [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), and he has much more stubble in the latter.

- Interestingly, John's revolver is able to instanteously kill mutant zombies with a headshot, a feat no firearm in Road of the Dead 2 can claim.
  - This is more of a running joke within the community than anything really interesting, as obviously it was for gameplay reasons because RotD would be unnecessarily much harder if mutants required multiple shots to be killed.
  - On the other hand, John's revolver may be of an absurdly large caliber (such as [.44 Magnum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.44_Magnum "wikipedia:.44 Magnum")) that may allow for such extreme damage.

- John Creasman was named after a voice actor of the same name, known on Newgrounds as [Xalkie](https://xalkie.newgrounds.com/), who has worked with [EvilDog Games](./evildog-games "EvilDog Games"). Ironically, he does not voice John, but rather the commander in both [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2") and [Captain Walker](./jerome-walker "Jerome Walker") in [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead").

- John Creasman and [his modified Mustang](./john-creasman-s-car "John Creasman's Car") might be partially inspired by [Marvin Heemeyer and the 2004 "Killdozer Incident" in Granby, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvin_Heemeyer "wikipedia:Marvin Heemeyer").
  - Both events involve a mechanic equipping a vehicle with improvised metal armor and using it for destructive action to defy the government.
  - Additionally, the [Military](./military "Military") dispatching [Hellfire](./hellfire "Hellfire") mirrors a considered scenario during the Killdozer rampage where the Colorado National Guard discussed sending an Apache with Hellfire missiles to destroy Heemeyer's bulldozer.
