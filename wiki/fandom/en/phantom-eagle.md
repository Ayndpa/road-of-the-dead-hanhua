**Phantom Eagle** is a [Bomber Jet](./bomber-jet "Bomber Jet") carrying a nuclear warhead, circling [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") during the incident. It is eventually responsible for the nuking of Evans City, witnessed during both [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2").

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

Phantom Eagle is ordered to circle Evans City at some point before [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") begins to make his escape through [Highway 65](./highway-65 "Highway 65"), as evidenced between dialogue between him and a soldier when John is idling in the garage. Considering how the soldier hopes the order to nuke the city won't be needed, Phantom Eagle is likely a last-ditch resort.

By the time John reaches the abandoned military checkpoint at West Ridge, [General Sherman](./general-sherman "General Sherman"), who had taken over the operation, informs the pilot that the President has approved a nuclear bombing against Evans City in a last-ditch effort to prevent the spread of the infection. Despite the fact that many [civilians](./civilians "Civilians") and [military personnel](./military "Military") are still inside the city, the order to nuke the city is given within minutes, leaving [almost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [nobody](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") a chance to escape.

The following level, Tunnel Drive, is John racing against time to get to the tunnel before the nuclear strike occurs, killing many civilians and military personnel who are yet to evacuate. It fails to exterminate the infection, which by this point managed to overrun many other cities.
