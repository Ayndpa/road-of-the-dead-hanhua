## Opening Crawl

(Day 1: Small isolated events of an unknown illness and violence are reported in and around Evans City.)

(Day 2: News reports of a man dying of an unknown illness who was rushed to Evans City hospital where bloody violence and chaos ensued.)

(Day 3: More reports of sick, seemingly dead people committing acts of random violence in the city. Cannibalism is also witnessed but grisly details are being withheld to reduce panic.)

(Day 4: The CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) moves in, setting up makeshift to try to contain the unknown illness. It's reported that bites and deep scratches can transfer the disease.

(The infected victims are clinically dead but still move and attack violently, in larger and larger numbers.)

(Day 5: All news channels are forced off the air. Military forces move in and begin a quarantine of the entire city. NO ONE is permitted to ENTER or LEAVE the city. Military officials broadcast the rules of the quarantine: to keep calm and stay indoors.

(All outside news channels are reporting that there is a chemical leak and that it's being cleaned up. The truth of the mysterious illness is being withheld from the public under government order.)

(Day 6: Riots and panics break out as people try to leave the city, hundreds are arrested and put into makeshift camps in local schools and jails. The military and CDC set up quarantine checkpoints throughout Evans City to uphold the quarantine. Churches and police stations are set up to be last minute evacuation zones.

(More people become infected and lash out violently. THOUSANDS of people are being killed. Military open fire on anyone approaching checkpoints. The CDC begins burning large piles of bodies.)

(Day 7: The infected walking corpses start to outnumber living people in the city. Checkpoints are being overrun at a faster and faster rate. A central command and communications checkpoint is overrun by a panicked riot of hundreds of civilians followed by a horde of undead. A high security broadcast radio is stolen in the chaos. Communication breaks down throughout military channels as the military is thrown into disarray.)

## Introduction Cutscene

(A military jet flies over the city. Pan to an overturned military Humvee that's covered in blood and spewing smoke. A wounded, blood soaked soldier grunts with exertion as he tries to crawl out from behind it. A zombie grabs him and pulls him back behind it.)

Soldier: No! No! No!

(The zombie is heard eating him. The camera pans to a female soldier, Diane, grappling with a large zombie.)

Diane: (Grunts) Shit!

(The zombie pins her to the ground, rips off her helmet, and snarls as it tries to bite her. Panicking, Diane notices a nearby pistol. Holding the zombie back with both hands and one foot, she grabs the gun and shoots it three times in the head, killing it. She kicks the body off her and rises to her feet. Meanwhile, a Humvee is racing in her direction. Diane notices three zombies shambling towards her from behind.)

Diane: Shit! Fuck!

(She aims at the zombies. The driver of the Humvee, Cocheta, puts the pedal to the metal and rams into the three zombies, sending body parts flying everywhere. He gets out of the car. A wounded zombie that has lost the entire lower half of its body crawls towards him, hissing. Cocheta finishes it with his Pistol and turns to Diane.)

Cocheta: Come on, let's go! Move it!

Diane: (Shocked) Ye... yeah!

(She runs up to him as the screen fades to black.)
