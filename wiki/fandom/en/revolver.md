The **Revolver** is a weapon exclusively wielded by [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman") in *[Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead")*.

Used only as a last effort attempt at dispatching [clinging zombies](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") or [soldiers](./enemy-soldier "Enemy Soldier") at the cost of destroying your windshield, the Revolver is the only weapon the player can use to safely defend themselves without resorting to methods that would damage their car. However, the firearm has a limited amount of bullets and cannot be reloaded.

## Trivia

- It resembles a snub nose revolver.
- It isn't clear as to how Creasman acquired the revolver, since it instantly comes into his possession after purchasing the "firearm" upgrade.
  - There is a likelihood the Revolver was made by him, explaining why its design is snub-nosed and doesn't bear much of a resemblance to any real revolver, or was available before, kept as a personal defense weapon that was utilized during his escape.
- Interestingly enough, John Creasman sports multiple ammo belts (one around his leg and waist respectively) when the firearm upgrade is maxed out, but doesn't make use of them even after he runs out of ammo.
  - It's possible that the bullets do not fit his revolver and are merely there for aesthetic purposes.
