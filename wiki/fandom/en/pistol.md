## Overview

 ![Diane wielding the Pistol in the intro cutscene.](/fandom-img/Diane_s_Pistol.png) 

Diane wielding the Pistol in the intro cutscene.

 

The **Pistol** is a weapon appearing in [Road Of The Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead") and [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2"). In the first game it is used as a last effort attack by [soldiers](./enemy-soldier "Enemy Soldier") clinging to the hood of [Creasman's car.](./john-creaseman-s-car "John Creaseman's Car") In the second game it is available to Diane and Cocheta from the beginning of the game.

It is effective against the common undead but it shouldn't be used to fight stronger zombies like the ravager or the brute. It has unlimited spare ammo but you have to reload.

Like all other weapons it can be upgraded at police stations or ammo crates. It has little recoil. In both games it is also used by soldiers if they cling to our hood.

## Trivia

- This weapon is based on a [Beretta M9 pistol.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beretta_M9) It can be also seen in undead police officers holsters, and also in SWAT Zombies holsters and Riot Control zombies holsters and finally in undead soldiers holsters.
