Many of you guys who played Lab of the Dead noticed that the plot is looking similar to the film - The Day of the Dead (1985). I did the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qu9dljtqCMM) about similarity of the events of the game to the film, so if you are interested you may enjoy.

 ![Dr](/fandom-img/Interrogation.png) 

**Dr. Logan and Captain Henry Rhodes**

 

In the Lab of the Dead game Dr. Allen Creasman Tyler murdering his team and then using their corpses to feed the zombies; In the film 'The Day of the Dead' we are having 3 people that were exploring behavior of the zombies. It's: Dr. Matthew Logan (scientist), Dr. Ted Fisher (scientist), Sarah Bowman (assistant). Dr. Matthew Logan is the only one who really doing progress with exploring, so Matthew Logan actually our Allen Creasman; Matthew Logan was using the corpses of his team from the beginning. He was doing it secretly until he was caught by Captain Henry Rhodes, when he was giving meat to the zombie Captain Henry Rhodes with his soldiers were watching it, then they started interrogating the Dr. Logan, until they opened freezing room and discovered the corpse of their former soldier. In a raged frenzy Captain Henry Rhodes refuses to listen Dr. Logan's excuses and pulls his automatic rifle m16 and shoots him.

In the film and the game scientists argues with the soldiers about the experiments, because it took a long time for exploring and it show the lack of the results. Arguments of soldiers were that they are risking their lives going outside to keep them safe, while the arguments of scientist were to explore and try to make zombies them behave loyal to the human. In the game Allen murdering his team, while they were sleeping and then used their corpses for feeding; In the film Dr. Logan was using corpses from the beginning, doing it secretly until he was caught by soldiers and killed.
