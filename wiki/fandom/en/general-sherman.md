**General Sherman** is a recurring character in the Saga of the Dead, appearing as a minor, unseen antagonist in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead"), and a supporting character in [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2").

## Road of the Dead

Sherman does not appear in Road of the Dead physically, but his voice can be heard over the radio shortly after encountering Mutants as he takes charge of the operation in Evans City. He immediately calls for the complete evacuation of the city before ordering a nuclear strike. He orders repeated attacks on [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), but eventually calls off the mission moments before the nuclear strike.

## Road of the Dead 2

In his attempts to secure a transport, [Cocheta Solomon](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") manages to reach General Sherman's helicopter, Flyboy 1, though General wants them to wait. Aware of the imminent nuclear strike, Cocheta angrily cuts Sherman off and calls him out for trying to nuke Evans City. Sherman admits his failure, but denies the evac request as they are too far away. Undeterred, Cocheta suggests both parties to converge in Fairview Mall's parking lot and questions Sherman's morality. Sherman relents and makes it clear that he will leave them behind if they do not arrive on time.

Flyboy 1 lands sometime before Diane and Cocheta's arrival, and Sherman is annoyed at their absence, but keeps his promise and lets his men defend the helicopter. When they show up, he opens the helicopter's side door and ushers them inside, then orders an immediate takeoff. His pilot, Baliski, denies, citing hydraulic faults and requests five minutes for repairs. With their time running out, Sherman gives him only two while Diane and Cocheta fight off the zombies. It leaves and successfully avoids the blast.

After going airborne, Sherman learns the nuke failed to wipe out most of the zombies. He remains confident, but Diane and Cocheta both question its effectiveness, with the latter trying to attack him. His attempts to contact units under his command fall under dead ears, with the HQ ultimately overrun, much to his dismay. He is, however, able to reach unit Caspar and orders a retreat before explaining to Diane that the whole country was overwhelmed quickly with their assets spread thin. After getting a status report from Baliski regarding their operating range, Sherman proposes regrouping with Caspar near the town of alliance, where they will try to hold out. Out of options, everyone else on board agree with his plan. He then directs Caspar to also move there before swearing revenge on the zombies.
