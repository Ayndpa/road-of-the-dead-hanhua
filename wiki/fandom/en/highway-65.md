Highway 65 is the main location in [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of the Dead") and a visited location in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"). The highway is seen as being in the air, sustained by pillars. The highway is filled with abandoned cars, checkpoints, and zombies. Interestingly, military checkpoints are only seen as operational on the highway, as any others are overrun.

Highway 65 is a highway that runs through the entirety of [Evans City,](./evans-city "Evans City") providing a faster means of transportation compared to roads (this is just an assumption). The building generation is different from roads, as instead of small buildings, you usually have tall towers, or occasionally small shops or buildings that generate outside of the road.

This area is also available for use in user campaigns in Road of the Dead 2 by navigating to Patterns> New> Highway.
