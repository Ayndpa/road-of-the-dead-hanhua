The **S14 Virus** outbreak is the main focus of the [Saga of the Dead](./saga-of-the-dead-wiki "Saga of The Dead Wiki") series. The blood-bound virus turns anyone it infects into cannibalistic mindless zombies who in turn bite and infect others, spreading the virus very quickly. Majority of its origin is revealed through [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead").

## Timeline

The S14 Virus was designed as a bioweapon by the [US Military](./military "Military") to effectively clear out rogue cities and countries in a matter of days. [Alpha Team](./alpha-team "Alpha Team"), which had created it, was in charge of inventing a failsafe in the form of the S15 Virus. All work had been done in a secret underground facility near [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"). [Dr Lori Benedict](./lori-benedict "Lori Benedict") was in charge of the S15 virus's production, under the guise of developing a mood control method instead of a bioweapon failsafe.

Unfortunately, Alpha Team never managed to finish the S15 due to sheer negligence causing them to disregard safety protocols. During a regular patrol through the specimen storage room, Private Peter Johnson strayed too close to the specimens and gets scratched. Initially he tries to hide it, and in his fourth (and final human) log brushes it off as a minor cut. As the staff logs were being reviewed by Lori per protocol, she realises Peter is infected and orders a facility wide search for him. Unfortunately, the private managed to contaminate the entire north wing of the facility, which was overrun. As Lori became aware of the project's reality, she decides to terminate it and was killed for treason. Captain Walker, the leader of all forces in the facility, failed to contain the zombies, who presumably killed him and his men before escaping to Evans City.

[Road of the Dead 2's](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2") loading crawl explains majority of what happens after the zombies make it to Evans City.

First it started with isolated events throughout the city, then a infected patient started a major outbreak in a hospital. The CDC and US Military move in on the fourth day of the outbreak, cutting off the media and begin evacuation. But the zombies have already grown in numbers. By day 6, the city is completely lockdowned and quarantined. By day 7, it is all but lost. Military checkpoints are being overrun at a faster and faster rate. Military outposts and HQs are overrun by civillans and zombies.

By this point, some zombies have already escaped the quarantine and started outbreaks in other cities. Nukes are dropped all over the country but to little effect as the zombies are too widespread to be completely wiped out. Civilian casualities are skyrocketing as the days go on, and so are the zombie's numbers.

## The Virus

The S14 virus is 100% lethal meaning that any attempt to prevent or slow it down once it has contacted the body is useless. After termination, the zombies' corpses can still be virus carriers, meaning the bodies must be completely burnt to eliminate the chance of post-mortem infection. According to Lori's documentation, the virus leads to the death of a specimen, but after death, the virus reactivates the brain and slows down or even stops decaying. The virus resides in an infected individual's fluids such as blood or saliva, hence why scratches and bites are carriers for infection. Infected saliva or blood entering an uninfected's blood will result in infection. Once infected and revived, the specimen is unable to feel pain and its body becomes less responsive against pain. A zombie will essentially keep going unless its brain is completely destroyed or its body is completely mutilated.

Viruses, being (particularly) living things, tend to mutate, and the S14 virus is no exception. In fact, the S14 virus seems to mutate far faster than the regular infectious pathogen, as zombies were seen mutated by the end of the 1st week of ROTD and ROTD 2. The mutation appears to hone the physical strength of its host by making it stronger and faster. The [ravagers](./ravager "Ravager") were the first case, slightly mutated to be stronger than the common dead. [Brutes](./brutes "Brutes") showed up second, using the inherent strength and health of its host, to be far stronger than the regular zombies. Eventually, the brutes gave way to the [mutants](./mutant-zombies "Mutant Zombies") and their [alpha](./alpha-mutants "Alpha Mutants") counterparts. It should be noted that the mutations seem to affect intelligence as well as mutants and alphas show more advanced strategies and tactics than the common dead, with the Alphas leading small packs of regular mutants.

Speaking of undead intelligence, infected specimens do seem to retain specific memories and brain function, which can be controlled to a certain extent. This possibility was explored by scientist [Allen C. Tyler](./allen-c-tyler "Allen C. Tyler") in [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead").
