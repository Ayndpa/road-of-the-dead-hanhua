**Cocheta Solomon** is one of the two protagonists of [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2").

## Road of the Dead 2

Cocheta Solomon is a NCO (non-commissioned officer, rank of around sergeant to sergeant major) of the [US Military](./military "Military") stationed with Unit Delta-2 until the rest of the team perished. Growing disillusioned with the military's efforts, he deserts, hoping to flee [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") by going west. On his way out, he happens to encounter and rescue [Diane Roseworth](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth"), the other protagonist of the game and the last survivor of Kilo-5. Cocheta convinces Diane to join him, believing merely following orders is a death sentence.

After making it through the overrun streets of Evan City to Madera District, a [Hellfire support chopper](./hellfire "Hellfire") is able to contact them, informing them that the West side is completely overrun and military evacuation attempts are being made on Highway 65's Chinatown exit. The chopper provides air support for a short time before having to regroup with the evacuation transport. Cocheta and Diane head for Chinatown only to find that all the transport have already left. It is around this time where they intercept a radio transmission from General Sherman ordering a nuclear strike on the city. This causes Cocheta to panic, as they cannot hope to reach the tunnel in time. As he desperately calls assistance to no avail while Diane berates him, he decides to leave the Highway, determined to find another evacuation helicopter. He eventually reaches Flyboy 1, General Sherman's helicopter, who advises him to wait for the next transport. Knowing they will never get one, a furious Cocheta suggests they could converge for a timely pickup, and calls out Sherman for leaving them to perish in the nuclear blast, with the General ultimately conceding.

Diane and Cocheta manage to make it through Northside park to Fairview Mall. They defend Flyboy 1 as an emergency repair is performed to get it airborne and evade the nuclear blast in time, only to learn it was ineffective. When Sherman refuses to admit defeat, Cocheta tries to attack him, only to be restrained by two other soldiers. Cocheta continues to call out Sherman's vain attempts of contacting units that have perished under his command in the nuclear strike. Out of options, he, Diane and the rest agree to continue with Sherman's plan as they fly off to regroup with Casper, Sherman's only remaining unit.

## Weapons

In [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2"), both Cocheta and Diane have a different list of weapons they can choose from. Most of them have a greater emphasis on raw burst firepower at the expense of long reload times.

If Cocheta is chosen as the gunner, he will have access to the Pistol and Assault SMG. He can later obtain the Auto Shotgun and LMG using RP, the ingame currency.

## Trivia

- Cocheta is noted to have a decisive, aggressive personality.
  - He is very firm and stubborn in his decision to go west, even after being informed of its infeasibility multiple times. When that fails, he insists on finding an airlift, and repeatedly talks Diane out of despair when she resigns to their circumstances.
  - He is enraged at General Sherman's last-minute decisions and orders that led to many otherwise avoidable deaths, and directly talks down Sherman regarding this, causing Sherman to change his mind and help them escape.
- Cocheta and his unit Delta-2 was deployed very recently, presumably on Day 4-6 of the fall of Evans City.
- Cocheta wears his helmet and mask for majority of the game and only removes it while on the helicopter.
- Cocheta makes a notable comment on how the zombie invasion is a punishment for mankind's selfish behavior, but also notes that "God has nothing to do with this" when Diane expresses relief after they establish contact with Flyboy 1. While he did not elaborate on either, he was partially correct regarding the origin of the zombies, as the [S14 Virus](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus") was indeed engineered as a bioweapon.
