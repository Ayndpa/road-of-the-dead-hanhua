Garages

 

## General Information

Garages are the only means of vehicle repair available in [Road of The Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of The Dead 2"), and vehicle performance upgrades can be purchased there.

## Description

Car repair garages can be found in many parts of [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City"). These buildings were places of business by mechanics, to repair customers cars. Now abandoned, these places contain many spare tires, repair equipment and extra parts for bumper fortification for your own [vehicle](./humvee "Humvee") at the cost of RP.
