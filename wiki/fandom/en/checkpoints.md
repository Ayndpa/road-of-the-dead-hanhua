Checkpoints were made to stop the zombie advance and to stop civilians. All of them got over run and destroyed when the nuke fell. There were 10 checkpoints on Highway 65 in ROTD 1 (the last without any soldiers and overrun by undead hordes). In ROTD 2 you encounter many over run checkpoints with many undead soldiers. Highway checkpoints:

Checkpoint 1: The smallest checkpoint. Has very few soldiers and sometimes none. This is where the military first noticed John.

Checkpoint 2: Second smallest checkpoint. More soldiers here.

Checkpoint 3: This checkpoint is harder and a bit longer. Many moving soldiers. After you pass this one spike non moving strips and adjusting soldiers will appear. You also get identified on this checkpoint.

Checkpoint 4: This is the checkpoint where there even more soldiers and a lot of spike strips and adjusting soldiers. After you pass this one Hellfire will come after you and moving spike strips will appear. Also hiding soldiers will appear.

Checkpoint 5: Even more soldiers. Barely was holding out the zombies until John came. He destroyed the fences and the checkpoint got over run. After you pass this one bomb squads will appear.

Checkpoint 6: Similar number of soldiers to Checkpoint 5. After John passes this checkpoint, General Sherman will come on the radio and start giving orders to remaining military units. Mutated zombies will appear, and jets will begin to carpet bomb the highway.

Checkpoint 7: More soldiers. Despite orders from Sherman to let John through, they still shoot at John. Once John has driven through the checkpoint, surviving soldiers leave to regroup at the Iachi River.

Checkpoint 8: Slightly more soldiers, and last organised stand against John. After this checkpoint, Hellfire units will use air-to-ground missiles to take down John.

Checkpoint 9: The last checkpoint that has soldiers. They were ordered by Sherman to retreat, but chose to fight John instead. Beyond this checkpoint, Hellfire units and jets will no longer come after you, and highway soldiers will be gone.

Checkpoint 10: Units here were evacuated and sent to the Iachi River. Once John arrives, there are no soldiers, and zombies have begun to fill the checkpoint.
