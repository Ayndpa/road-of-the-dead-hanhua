**Moving Cars** are neutral vehicles encountered in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), driven by panicked [civilians](./civilians "Civilians") attempting to escape [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City").

## Gameplay

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

When approaching an intersection, there is a chance that a car will spawn from one end of the intersection and drive towards the opposite end. Telegraphed by the sounds of their horn and the driver panicking, these cars are indifferent to the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") but may still collide with them out of coincidence.

To avoid them, simply pay attention to what side of the intersection the vehicle is coming from, and dodge their vehicle. Firing at them is a waste of ammunition, especially that of the machinegun and rocket turrets.

## Appearances

### [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2")

Moving cars make their only appearances in [Road of the Dead 2](./road-of-the-dead-2-3 "Road of the Dead 2"), its gamemodes, and the level editor.

These vehicles were used by civilians as a last resort to escape from the [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") and would occasionally be encountered by the [Lost](./diane-roseworth "Diane Roseworth") [Guns](./cocheta-solomon "Cocheta Solomon") as moving obstacles.

#### Survival Guide Description

As order has broken down and the quarantine has failed, many [civilians](./civilians "Civilians") have panicked and broken out of their designated zones, using their own vehicles.

When driving through intersections, be watchful of these incoming cars, they can come from the right or left side, usually blaring their horn at you.
