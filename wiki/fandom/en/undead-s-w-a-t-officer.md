The Undead S.W.A.T. Officer is a type of zombie in Road of the Dead 2. Before dying and reanimating as zombies, these men served in the Evans City Police Department S.W.A.T. team. They tried to contain the outbreak along with other offices, CDC officials, scientists, and National Guard personnel. However, it is possible that the SWAT officers were actually the first to actively combat the undead but did not have much, success in fighting because of the police department's lack of knowledge when fighting zombies and the extremely large numbers of infected. After the CDC's and police's failed attempt to contain the outbreak, the National Guard moved in and quarantined the city, and the remains of the police department and CDC in Evans City were mobilized to help the National Guard maintain the quarantine around the city. They are stronger than Undead Riot Control Officers, but still weaker than Undead Soldiers. They don't actually appear in the game possibly due to a lack of zombie models.
