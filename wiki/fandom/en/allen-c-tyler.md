**Allen Tyler Creasman** is the protagonist of [Lab of the Dead](./lab-of-the-dead-2 "Lab of the Dead") and [Range of the Dead](./range-of-the-dead-2 "Range of the Dead") games. He is also the brother of [John Creasman](./john-creasman "John Creasman"), the protagonist of [Road of the Dead](./road-of-the-dead-2 "Road of The Dead").

## Appearances

### Lab of the Dead

Allen is a surviving scientist native to [Evans City](./evans-city "Evans City") who managed to join up with a surviving group of military personnel led by Captain Wood, and the group manage to bunker in an abandoned research facility which strangely contains a storage room full of zombies. As the sole scientist of the group, Allen takes it upon himself to analyse the zombies' psychology, from a safe distance. Having lost hope on a cure, Allen is determined to instead experiment with the zombies' reactions, believing their behavior might be controllable. Fully investing into his own research, Allen uses up considerable amounts of the limited resources the group has for his side projects, causing further frustration within the group. He also ends up recovering information regarding the [Alpha Team](./alpha-team "Alpha Team"), the previous occupants of the facility, and learns the truth behind the [outbreaks](./zombie-virus "Zombie Virus").

Eventually, Captain Wood grows tired of Allen, having (correctly) assumed all of the scientist's experiments are worthless with the entire country now overrun. Wood's paranoia of Allen reaches a breaking point three months in, where he, after noticing information regarding the Alpha Team, lashes out at Allen, destroys one of his specimens and demands him to leave. Fearing for his life and work, Allen strikes first and kills everyone with lethal injections during their sleep, allowing him to later use their flesh to his own ends. He quickly regrets the decision as Captain Wood was proven right, with nothing coming out of it. Allen resolves to eventually leave and survive on his own.

### Range of the Dead

Allen has left the facility to find the nearby town of [Senoia](./senoia "Senoia"). Before he can make the perilous journey however, he must first hone his rifle and bow skills by shooting the zombies in the valley below the facility. After shooting 100 zombies at different distances and varying wind, Allen believes himself to be ready, and is alerted by a passing [attack helicopter](./hellfire "Hellfire"). Realizing it may lead to potential survivors, Allen sets off to find them.

## Trivia

- Allen's final log reveals his middle name to be Creasman and mentioned his brother in the one preceding it, indicating he and John are indeed related.
  - Allen makes no indication that he and John are anything less than full-blooded siblings. The odd naming convention of Tyler's full name could be explained by their parents separating, with the mother taking custody of Tyler and making him use either her maiden name or the last name of her new partner. This may explain his negative opinion of John.
  - According to promotional material from Evil-Dog for the release of Road of the Dead 2, Allen is 32 years old, making him the younger brother. It also specifies that his scientific field, being a biochemist, as well as a psychologist. Curiously, the poster transposes his name as "Allen Tyler Creasman".
- Allen reveals small tidbits about his background through his notes on various items.
  - As later evidenced in Range of the Dead, he seems to have limited experience with guns. Allen says that he never fired the gun given to him by Wood before arriving at the complex and laments that he does not know how to assemble an AK-47, but he either has basic firearms training or is a quick learner, as he has no problem using weapons like the hunting rifle, shotgun, or machine gun being barely affected by recoil.
  - Allen mentions that he was a good baseball player when he was young and shows decent form while swinging the bat.
  - He seems to have something of a sweet tooth, as he mentions that he kept samples of the pie and chocolate bar for himself.
  - Allen appears wary of food contamination, citing bad experiences with poor quality kebab and expired eggs.
  - Allen has a love for dogs and is dismayed to make use of them in the experiments, though he seems rather amused when a zombie punches a small dog off its hand.
